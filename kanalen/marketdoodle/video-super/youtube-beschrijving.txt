You wake up and your little sister is floating above the kitchen table. Dad just walked through the wall. Mom makes breakfast in one second. Everyone got a superpower overnight... except you.

This story is fiction. The real fact: about 1 in 4 people are "supertasters" who taste bitter food, like broccoli, much more strongly (research by Linda Bartoshuk, Yale).
