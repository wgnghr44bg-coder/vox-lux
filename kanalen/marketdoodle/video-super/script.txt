# What If Everyone Got Superpowers Overnight? — ± 3 min liggend (eigenaar 23 nov: "ja is goed"). Les uit Studio: meteen chaos in de eerste 5 s, regels daarna.
# Alleen tonen wat gezegd wordt. Echt feit: ongeveer 1 op de 4 mensen is een "supertaster" (onderzoek Linda Bartoshuk, Yale): bitter (zoals broccoli) smaakt veel sterker.
N|keuken|It's 7:02. Your little sister is floating above the kitchen table. And Dad just walked through the wall.
DAD|keuken|Morning! Has anyone seen my keys?
N|keuken|Mom makes breakfast. All of it. In one second.
YOU|keuken|Okay. What is going on?
N|titel|So, what if everyone got superpowers overnight?
N|keuken|Rules. Everyone wakes up with one power. Nobody gets to choose. And nobody knows how long it lasts.
N|keuken|Your sister can fly. Dad walks through walls. Mom is super fast.
N|keuken|And you? You jump. You push the wall. You stare at a spoon for a full minute.
N|keuken|Nothing. You're the only one with no power at all.
YOU|keuken|This is so unfair.

N|feit|And here's something real. Some people already have a tiny superpower.
