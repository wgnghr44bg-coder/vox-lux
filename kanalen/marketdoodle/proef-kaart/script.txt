@scene a
You buy an Nvidia graphics card, the RTX fifty ninety, for nineteen hundred ninety-nine dollars.
Who gets your money? Let's follow every hundred dollars you pay.
@scene b
First, a warning. Nvidia does not say what it makes on one card.
So these are estimates, and we show where each one comes from.
@scene c
The heart of the card is one big chip, made by TSMC in Taiwan.
Tom's Hardware estimates it costs about two hundred ninety dollars to make. That is about fifteen dollars of your hundred.
@scene d
But Nvidia usually does not build the card itself. Brands like ASUS and MSI do that, with memory, a cooler, and a board.
And those card makers keep very little. About five dollars of every hundred, Jon Peddie Research estimated.
@scene e
So where does the rest go? Memory, parts, shipping, the shop, and Nvidia's own price for the chip.
Nvidia keeps that split a secret. But one number is official.
@scene f
Across the whole company, Nvidia keeps almost seventy-five dollars of every hundred, after the cost of making its products.
Most of that comes from AI chips for data centers, not from gaming.
@scene g
Here is the receipt for your card.
Making the chip is the cheap part. Designing it, that is where Nvidia earns its money.
