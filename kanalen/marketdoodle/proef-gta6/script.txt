# Proef v2 "GTA 6: je koopt het in de winkel - wie verdient er nu aan?" (22 nov 2026). Eigenaar: "leuker brengen, naar de winkel, kopen, dan de vraag".
# SCHATTINGEN (bron in beeld): verdeling fysiek spel volgens analist Serkan Toto (2020): winkel ~30%, licentie platform ~15%, schijf/doos/vervoer ~5%, uitgever rest.
#  Op $79,99: winkel 24,00 | Sony 12,00 | schijf 4,00 | Take-Two 39,99. Digitaal: Sony 30% = 24,00 -> Take-Two 55,99.
# Prijs Standard $79,99 (Variety). Ontwikkelkosten ~ $1-2 mld (schattingen, GamesRadar/NME) -> 25-50 mln stuks bij $40.
# Dag 1 ~19 mln (Sensor Tower, schatting). GTA V >230 mln (Take-Two).
@scene a
It's launch night. You walk into a game store, and there it is. Grand Theft Auto Six.
You take it to the counter. Seventy-nine ninety-nine.
And as you pay, you start to wonder. How much are they actually earning on my purchase?
@scene b
Let's start right where you are standing. The store.
Analysts estimate a shop keeps about thirty percent of a new game. That is about twenty-four dollars.
@scene c
But this is a PlayStation disc. And Sony wants its share too.
For every disc sold, the publisher pays Sony a license fee. Around fifteen percent, or twelve dollars.
@scene d
Then the disc itself, the box, and the trip from the factory to the shop. About four dollars.
So from your seventy-nine ninety-nine, Take-Two, the owner of Rockstar Games, gets about forty dollars.
@scene e
And that is not profit yet. First, it has to earn back what the game cost to make.
Rockstar never said what it cost. Estimates run from one to two billion dollars.
At forty dollars a copy, that is twenty-five to fifty million copies, just to break even.
@scene f
On day one, Sensor Tower estimates around nineteen million copies were sold. And Grand Theft Auto Five sold more than two hundred thirty million.
Now here is a fun twist. If you had bought it digitally, there is no shop and no disc. Sony takes thirty percent, and Take-Two gets about fifty-six dollars.
@scene g
So here is the receipt for your copy from the store.
Twenty-four for the shop, twelve for Sony, four for the disc, and about forty for Take-Two. And the more people buy it, the more of that they keep.
