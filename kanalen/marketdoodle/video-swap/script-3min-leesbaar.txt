WHAT IF YOU SWAPPED LIVES WITH YOUR DAD FOR A WEEK?
(± 3 minuten, alleen jij en papa ruilen, iedereen houdt zijn eigen stem)

VERTELLER: It's Monday, 7 a.m. You wake up. But the bed is huge. And there's a tie on the door.
VERTELLER: You look in the mirror. And your dad is looking back at you.
JIJ (jongensstem): Oh no. Oh no, no, no.
VERTELLER: In your room, someone screams. It's your body. With your dad inside.
PAPA (mannenstem): Why am I so short?
VERTELLER: So, what if you swapped lives with your dad for a week?
VERTELLER: Okay, rules. Seven days. Then everything goes back.
VERTELLER: You do his job. He goes to your school. And nobody can know.
PAPA (mannenstem): How hard can school be?
JIJ (jongensstem): How hard can a job be?
VERTELLER: Monday. Dad's office. Your own desk. Free coffee. This is great.
VERTELLER: Then you open his computer. Two hundred and forty emails. All urgent.
BAAS: I need that report by three.
JIJ (jongensstem): What's a report?
VERTELLER: You have no idea. So you just nod. A lot.
VERTELLER: Meanwhile, your dad is in your class. Pop quiz. Fractions.
VERTELLER: He hasn't done fractions in twenty-five years. Four out of ten.
PAPA (mannenstem): This is harder than my job!
VERTELLER: Tuesday. The bills come in. Rent. The car. Food. The lights. The phone.
VERTELLER: You add it all up. And almost all of Dad's money is already gone.
JIJ (jongensstem): Wait. Where did all the money go?
VERTELLER: Wednesday. Mom sends you to the store with a list. You come home with four pizzas. And no milk.
VERTELLER: Thursday. Your boss is back.
BAAS: Where's the report?
JIJ (jongensstem): My dog ate it.
BAAS: You don't have a dog.
VERTELLER: And here's the real part. The US government added up what it costs to raise one kid, until age seventeen.
VERTELLER: About two hundred thirty-three thousand dollars. For one kid.
JIJ (jongensstem): Maybe I should stop asking for new sneakers.
VERTELLER: Friday night. You both sit in the kitchen. Too tired to sleep.
PAPA (mannenstem): I forgot how hard school is.
JIJ (jongensstem): And I didn't know how hard your job is.
ZUSJE: Why are you two acting so weird?
VERTELLER: For the first time, you really get each other.
VERTELLER: Monday, 7 a.m. The week is over. You close your eyes. You count to three.
VERTELLER: You open your eyes. You look down. And you're still in Dad's body.
VERTELLER: Then the doorbell rings. It's Dad's boss.
VERTELLER: And if anyone finds out, the swap stays forever.
