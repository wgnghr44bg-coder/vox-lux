What if every single person on Earth woke up with $1,000,000 in their bank account tomorrow morning?

Day one is amazing. Sports cars in every driveway, a boat that will never see water, and a horse named Mister Pancake. Day two is weird. And by day thirty… well. Milk costs $500, nobody wants to work, the lights go out, and a loaf of bread is suddenly worth more than a million dollars.

This actually happened, sort of: in 2009, Zimbabwe printed a single banknote worth one hundred trillion dollars, and it still couldn't buy a week of groceries. When everyone has more money, money simply stops being worth anything.
