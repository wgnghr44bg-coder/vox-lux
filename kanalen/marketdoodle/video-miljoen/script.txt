# What If Everyone on Earth Got $1 Million Tonight? — lang, minimaal 8 min (eigenaar 24 nov), liggend, GEEN muziek.
# Lessen AI Studio (24 nov): meteen chaos (geen regels/titelkaart vooraan), laten zien i.p.v. vertellen, steeds gekker,
# feit IN het verhaal, cliffhanger. Stiltes opvangen met strakke timing, echte geluidseffecten en steeds beweging in beeld.
# Past bij de Short die werkt (Everyone Earns, geld) -> die Short koppelen als Gerelateerde video.
# Echt feit: Zimbabwe gaf in 2009 een biljet van 100 biljoen Zimbabwaanse dollar uit (Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe), door hyperinflatie.
# Formaat: SPREKER|scène|tekst. Sprekers: N (verteller), YOU, DAD, MOM, SIS, BOSS, TEACH. Scènes = achtergronden (lijst onderaan).
YOU|slaapkamer-jij|Mom! Dad! Check your phones! Now!
N|slaapkamer-jij|It's 6 a.m. Your phone won't stop buzzing. You open your bank app. You blink. You look again.
N|slaapkamer-jij|One million dollars. In your account. Yesterday you had twelve dollars and a coupon for free fries.
YOU|slaapkamer-jij|One million dollars?! No way!
N|keuken|You run downstairs. Dad is staring at his phone. Mom is staring at hers. Your little sister is staring at hers.
SIS|keuken|I'm a millionaire! I'm seven!
MOM|keuken|This has to be a mistake. Banks don't just do this.
DAD|keuken|It's on the news. Everybody got it. Every single person on Earth. One million dollars. Overnight.
N|keuken|Nobody knows where it came from. Nobody cares. You're rich. Everyone is rich.
N|keuken|So, what happens next? Day one is amazing. Day two is weird. And by day thirty... well. Let's see.
N|straat|Day one. Your street looks like a car show. Your neighbor bought a sports car. In pink.
N|straat|The guy across the street bought three. He can only drive one at a time. He doesn't care.
N|straat|Dad orders a boat. You don't live near water.
DAD|straat|It's for the driveway. It's a driveway boat.
MOM|straat|You can't even swim.
DAD|straat|I don't need to swim. I'm a millionaire.
N|slaapkamer-jij|You buy every game ever made. Plus a gaming chair that cost more than your old house.
N|slaapkamer-jij|It has massage, cooling, and a tiny fridge in the armrest. You will never leave this chair.
SIS|slaapkamer-jij|I bought a horse. He's in the backyard. His name is Mister Pancake.
N|tuin|Mister Pancake eats Mom's flowers. Then the fence. Then the doormat.
MOM|tuin|Who sold a seven-year-old a horse?
SIS|tuin|A millionaire did.
N|school|Day two. You go to school. Nobody else does. Not even the teacher.
N|school|There's a note on the door. "Gone to Hawaii. Forever."
YOU|school|Best. Day. Ever.
N|school|You sit alone in an empty classroom for ten minutes. Just to feel it. Then you go home and play games for nine hours.
N|kantoor|Dad goes to work out of habit. The office is empty. His boss is wearing a crown.
BOSS|kantoor|I quit. You quit. Everybody quit.
DAD|kantoor|But who runs the company?
BOSS|kantoor|Who cares? I'm buying an island. A small one. Okay, a medium one.
N|kantoor|And that's the problem. Nobody wants to work anymore. Not the bus driver. Not the cook. Not the guy who fixes the internet.
N|straat|Day three. The garbage truck doesn't come. Neither does the mail. Or the pizza guy.
N|straat|Your street now has seven sports cars, one boat, one horse, and a mountain of trash bags.
DAD|straat|Somebody should really pick that up.
N|straat|Everybody looks at somebody else. Nobody picks it up.
N|straat|By the end of the day, the trash pile is taller than the boat. A family of raccoons moves in. They look very happy.
N|tv|Day four. You turn on the TV. A famous movie star is crying on the news.
N|tv|Nobody wants her autograph anymore. Everyone already has a mansion. Being rich isn't special when everybody is.
YOU|tv|Huh. I feel kind of bad for her.
SIS|tv|I don't. I have a horse.
N|keuken|Day five. The internet goes down. Your sister screams like the world is ending.
SIS|keuken|My tablet! Somebody fix it!
DAD|keuken|We'll pay someone. We're millionaires.
N|keuken|Dad calls a repair guy. The repair guy is also a millionaire. He's on a yacht. He laughs. He hangs up.
N|keuken|Dad calls ten more. Same answer. One of them is on the same yacht.
DAD|keuken|Okay. How hard can it be? I'll fix it myself.
N|keuken|Dad unplugs the router. Plugs it back in. Unplugs it again. Now the TV is broken too.
N|winkel|Day seven. Mom goes to the store for milk. A carton of milk now costs fifty dollars.
MOM|winkel|Fifty dollars? For milk?
N|winkel|The cashier shrugs. Everyone has a million dollars, so the store raised the prices. Then raised them again.
N|winkel|By lunch it's a hundred dollars. By dinner, two hundred. The price tags change while you're looking at them.
MOM|winkel|I'll just take one banana. How much is one banana?
N|winkel|The cashier says a number. Mom puts the banana back very, very slowly.
N|restaurant|Day eight. Mom has had enough. Tonight, the family goes to the fanciest restaurant in town. The one with the tiny food on big plates.
N|restaurant|The doors are open. The candles are lit. And there's nobody inside. No waiter. No chef. Nobody.
DAD|restaurant|Hello? We're millionaires! We'd like a table!
N|restaurant|Nobody answers. So Dad walks into the kitchen and puts on the chef's hat.
DAD|restaurant|How hard can it be?
N|restaurant|Forty minutes later, the smoke alarm goes off. Dinner is cereal. Fancy cereal. On a very big plate.
SIS|restaurant|This is the best restaurant ever.
N|restaurant|On the way out, Dad leaves a tip. One million dollars. There's nobody there to take it.
N|feit|And here's the crazy part. This has really happened. Not with free money, but with too much money.
N|feit|In Zimbabwe, the government printed so much money that prices went up every single day. Sometimes twice a day.
N|feit|In 2009, the country printed a single bill worth one hundred trillion dollars. And it still couldn't buy a week of groceries.
YOU|feit|So being a millionaire is only cool if nobody else is?
N|feit|Pretty much. Money is only worth something when there isn't too much of it.
N|keuken|Day ten. Dad holds a family meeting. He has a spreadsheet. Nobody asked for a spreadsheet.
DAD|keuken|The boat was two hundred thousand. The horse was fifty thousand. The gaming chair was... how much was the gaming chair?
YOU|keuken|I don't want to talk about the gaming chair.
DAD|keuken|And milk is now five hundred dollars.
SIS|keuken|Mister Pancake eats a lot.
N|keuken|And the stores are running out. The trucks stopped coming. Truck drivers are millionaires too.
N|keuken|The shelves get emptier every day. First the snacks. Then the bread. Then the toilet paper.
MOM|keuken|Not the toilet paper.
N|keuken|Yes. The toilet paper.
N|nacht|Day fourteen. The lights go out. The whole city. Pitch black.
N|nacht|The people who run the power plant? You guessed it. Island. Yacht. Hawaii.
YOU|nacht|I can't charge my phone. What do I do?
DAD|nacht|When I was a kid, we played board games.
N|nacht|So you play a board game. By candlelight. It's called Monopoly. Nobody finds it funny.
N|keuken|Day sixteen. Dad wakes up with a toothache. A big one. His face looks like a balloon.
DAD|keuken|I'll pay any dentist ten thousand dollars. Twenty thousand!
N|keuken|Ten thousand dollars now buys about three apples. Every dentist in town is on vacation.
N|keuken|Then Grandma shows up. With a piece of string and a door.
MOM|keuken|Mom, no.
N|keuken|Grandma ties one end to the tooth. And the other end to the door. She doesn't count to three.
N|keuken|The door slams. The tooth is out. Dad is fine. Mostly.
DAD|keuken|How much do I owe you?
N|keuken|Grandma wants one thing. A loaf of bread. And for the first time, you realize: bread might be worth more than a million dollars.
N|straat|Day twenty. The pink sports car is out of gas. The gas station is closed. The guy who owns it is on vacation.
N|straat|The whole street is full of expensive cars. None of them move. Everybody walks.
N|straat|And for the first time in weeks, people start talking to each other. About something weird.
N|straat|Who still knows how to do stuff?
N|straat|Turns out, a lot of people do. Your neighbor can fix pipes. Grandma can bake bread. The quiet guy at number twelve can fix the power.
N|straat|Your sister can ride a horse. That's suddenly very useful.
N|keuken|Nobody pays with money anymore. You trade. Bread for a pipe fix. Horse rides for eggs. Dad trades the boat for six chickens.
DAD|keuken|Best deal I ever made.
SIS|keuken|Mister Pancake is the richest one in the family now.
N|school|Day twenty-five. Your teacher comes back from Hawaii. She's sunburned and broke.
YOU|school|What happened to your million?
TEACH|school|I spent it on a coconut.
YOU|school|One coconut?
TEACH|school|It was a really good coconut. Now open your books.
N|school|And you know what? You're kind of happy to be back at school. Don't tell anyone.
N|school|Then she hands out homework. Okay. A little less happy.
N|kantoor|Dad goes back to the office. His boss is there too. No crown this time.
BOSS|kantoor|The island didn't have Wi-Fi. Or food. Or a toilet.
DAD|kantoor|So... back to work?
BOSS|kantoor|Back to work.
N|kantoor|Dad sits down at his desk. Two hundred and forty emails. He has never been this happy to see them.
N|keuken|Day thirty. Your family sits at the kitchen table. The boat is gone. The gaming chair is firewood.
DAD|keuken|We had a million dollars and now we're poorer than before.
MOM|keuken|At least we still have the horse.
SIS|keuken|And six chickens.
N|keuken|And you learned something. Money doesn't make bread. People make bread. Money is just how we say thank you.
N|keuken|Then, at midnight, every phone on Earth buzzes again. A new message.
N|keuken|"Thanks for testing. Round two starts tomorrow. This time, only one person gets the money."
N|keuken|Your phone buzzes one more time. It says, "Congratulations."
YOU|keuken|Oh no.
