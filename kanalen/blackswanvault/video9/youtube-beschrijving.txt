SpaceX shares closed at $127.79 on November 20, below the $135 price of its record IPO in June and about 40% below their peak. Three things are weighing on the stock: $40 billion of debt for AI chips that lenders are nervous about, an AI business that still loses money, and a lock-up that ends on December 8.
