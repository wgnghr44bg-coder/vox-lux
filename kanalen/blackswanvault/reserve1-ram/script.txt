# Titel (voorstel): Why RAM Got So Expensive. AI Is Eating Your Memory
# Opbouw: antwoord vooraan (AI-datacenters kopen het geheugen op), dan prijzen, waarom, wie verdient, wie betaalt, wanneer het stopt.
@scene open-prijs
If you tried to build a computer this year, you probably noticed something strange.
The memory, the RAM, suddenly cost more than almost anything else in the box.
A thirty-two gigabyte kit of fast memory now costs around four hundred fifty dollars.
Not long ago, the same kit cost about a hundred.
@scene open-antwoord
So what happened?
The short answer is artificial intelligence.
The companies that make memory chips are selling as much as they can to AI data centers.
And there is simply not enough left for the rest of us.
@scene open-geld
In the third quarter of this year, the whole memory industry brought in about two hundred billion dollars.
That is three hundred sixty-nine percent more than a year earlier.
But the number of chips they shipped grew by only about four percent.
Almost all of that extra money came from one thing: higher prices.
Let's see how we got here, who is making money, and when it might end.
@scene h1-wat
First, what is RAM?
It is your computer's short-term memory.
Every app you open, every browser tab, every game, lives in RAM while you use it.
Your phone has it, your laptop has it, your car has it.
And so does every AI server in the world.
@scene h1-drie
Almost all of the world's memory chips come from just three companies.
Samsung and SK Hynix in South Korea, and Micron in the United States.
When those three change their plans, the whole world feels it.
@scene h2-hbm
And this year, they changed their plans.
AI chips, like the ones Nvidia sells, need a special kind of memory.
It is called high bandwidth memory, or H B M.
It is stacked in layers, right next to the AI chip, so data can move incredibly fast.
@scene h2-ruil
H B M is much harder to make than normal memory.
It uses far more factory space for every gigabyte.
And it is far more profitable.
So the big three moved more and more of their factories to AI memory, and to memory for servers.
According to the research firm I D C, that came at the expense of the chips that go into laptops and phones.
@scene h3-prijzen
Then the prices exploded.
In the first quarter of this year, contract prices for memory almost doubled in just three months.
The research firm TrendForce called it a record for a single quarter.
And they kept climbing.
Up to twenty-three percent more in the third quarter.
And TrendForce expects up to eighteen percent more in the last quarter of the year.
@scene h3-winkel
In stores, you can see it on the price tags.
Storage is going up too.
According to Tom's Hardware, a two terabyte drive that cost eighty dollars this summer was back at one hundred thirty dollars in November.
