Jensen Huang is back as CEO of Nvidia, just 11 days after he resigned, and after President Trump helped bring him back. Investors cheered: on November 23, Nvidia added almost $1 trillion in market value in one day, by our calculation the biggest one-day gain in stock market history. But the board investigation, a California probe and 3 hearings in Washington are not over.
