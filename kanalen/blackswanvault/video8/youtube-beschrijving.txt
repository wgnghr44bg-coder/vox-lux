Bitcoin fell below $60,000 on November 20, more than 50% below its record of over $126,000. Four forces are selling at the same time: big investors leaving bitcoin funds, traders forced out of borrowed bets, a Fed that may raise rates again, and record AI stock debuts pulling money away. And this time, bitcoin fell much harder than the stock market.
