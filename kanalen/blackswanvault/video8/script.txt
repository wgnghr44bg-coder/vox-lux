# Titel (voorstel): Bitcoin Just Lost Half Its Value. Here's Who Is Selling
# Opbouw: antwoord vooraan (vier krachten tegelijk), dan val, ETF's, hefboom, Fed, AI-beursgangen, digitaal goud, bedrijven met bitcoin, wat nu.
@scene open-val
On Friday, November twentieth, bitcoin fell below sixty thousand dollars.
It was the first time since July.
Thirteen months ago, one bitcoin was worth more than one hundred twenty-six thousand dollars.
So in just over a year, it has lost more than half its value.
In the past month alone, it fell about twenty thousand dollars.
@scene open-weken
And it is not one bad day.
Bitcoin was heading for its seventh losing week in a row.
That has not happened since May 2022.
@scene open-antwoord
So who is selling?
The short answer is four groups at the same time.
Big investors are pulling money out of bitcoin funds.
Traders who borrowed to bet on bitcoin are being forced to sell.
The Federal Reserve may raise interest rates again.
And huge stock market debuts in AI are pulling money away from crypto.
@scene open-goud
There is one more problem.
Bitcoin was supposed to be digital gold, a safe place when markets fall.
This time, it fell much harder than the stock market.
Let's go through it, step by step.
@scene h1-funds
Start with the biggest buyers of the last two years.
In 2024, America approved funds that hold real bitcoin and trade on the stock market like a normal share.
They made it easy for anyone with a brokerage account to buy bitcoin.
And billions of dollars poured in.
@scene h1-uitstroom
Now that money is leaving.
On November seventeenth, these funds lost about two hundred forty million dollars.
The next day, about three hundred seven million.
And on November nineteenth, five hundred eighty-two million dollars in a single day.
@scene h1-blackrock
The biggest of them all is run by BlackRock, the largest asset manager in the world.
On November nineteenth, investors pulled five hundred forty-one million dollars out of BlackRock's bitcoin fund.
That was its largest one-day outflow since it launched.
Funds from Fidelity and ARK lost money on the same day too.
@scene h1-maand
And it was not a single bad day.
Money left these funds on twelve of the first fourteen trading days of November.
In total, almost three and a half billion dollars this month.
When the biggest buyers become sellers, the price has to find new buyers lower down.
Not everyone is selling.
On the same day that BlackRock's fund lost half a billion dollars, a newer bitcoin fund from Morgan Stanley took in about fifty million.
But that was a drop in the ocean.
@scene h2-hefboom
The second group is traders who used borrowed money.
On crypto exchanges, you can bet on bitcoin with ten, twenty, even one hundred times more money than you actually have.
That is called leverage.
If the price moves your way, you win big.
@scene h2-liquidatie
But if the price drops far enough, the exchange closes your bet automatically and sells.
This is called a liquidation.
And every forced sale pushes the price down a little further.
Which can trigger the next liquidation.
@scene h2-cijfers
In just twenty-four hours this week, about seven hundred million dollars in crypto bets were wiped out this way.
More than one hundred forty-two thousand traders were hit.
In bitcoin alone, about two hundred three million dollars were liquidated.
More than eighty percent of that came from traders betting the price would go up.
And the market has less cushion than before.
According to the site crypto dot news, the amount of buy and sell orders waiting on the big exchanges is about thirty-eight percent lower than before a hack at the exchange Binance in October.
In a thinner market, the same amount of selling moves the price further.
@scene h3-fed
The third force is the Federal Reserve.
Bitcoin did best when money was cheap and interest rates were low.
But right now, the Fed may be getting ready to raise rates again.
@scene h3-notulen
This week, the Fed published the minutes of its October meeting.
Almost all members said further rate increases would likely be appropriate.
The next meeting is on December eighth and ninth.
@scene h3-dollar
Higher rates make safe investments, like government bonds, pay more.
And they make risky bets, like bitcoin, less attractive.
At the same time, the US dollar is near its highest level in nineteen months.
When the dollar is strong, bitcoin is usually weak.
Washington has not helped either.
In September, a major crypto law, called the Clarity Act, failed in the Senate.
The crypto industry spent more than two hundred million dollars on this year's midterm elections.
According to CNBC, it got less out of that than in earlier elections.
@scene h4-ai
The fourth force is something new.
On November nineteenth, the AI company Anthropic went public.
It raised more than eighty-one billion dollars, one of the biggest stock market debuts ever.
In June, SpaceX had a giant debut of its own.
@scene h4-geld
To buy shares in these debuts, investors need cash.
And some of that cash came from selling other risky investments, like crypto.
The head of CoinShares, a large crypto investment firm, put it simply.
"AI is sucking out every marginal dollar away from crypto."
@scene h4-anthropic
Even the new shares did not escape the selling.
Anthropic's shares opened at one hundred eleven dollars and fifty cents, far above the eighty-three dollar offer price.
By the end of the first day, they had already fallen back to about ninety-seven.
Ripple's chief executive, Brad Garlinghouse, sees a pattern here.
After SpaceX went public in June, he said, crypto recovered once the new shares had been paid for.
He thinks the same could happen again.
@scene h5-goud
Now to the biggest question.
For years, bitcoin fans called it digital gold.
Something you buy when you do not trust the stock market.
@scene h5-test
This year, that idea was tested.
From October sixth to November eighteenth, the S and P five hundred fell about six percent.
In the same period, bitcoin fell about twenty-seven percent.
That is more than four times as much.
@scene h5-beurs
And on Friday, both fell together.
The Nasdaq one hundred dropped more than five percent in one day.
The S and P five hundred fell into what Wall Street calls a correction, more than ten percent below its peak.
And the VIX, Wall Street's fear gauge, jumped above thirty.
Bitcoin did not protect anyone.
It behaved like a risky tech stock.
@scene h5-echt-goud
Even real gold is not safe right now.
It is down about a third from its peak earlier this year.
On Friday alone, it fell more than four percent, to around three thousand five hundred fifteen dollars an ounce.
When everything falls at once, investors sell what they can, to raise cash.
The whole crypto market is now worth about two trillion dollars.
That is about twenty-seven percent less than just one month ago.
@scene h6-strategy
There is one company that sits at the center of all this.
It is called Strategy, and it used to be known as MicroStrategy.
For years, it borrowed money and sold shares, just to buy bitcoin.
@scene h6-cijfers
According to its latest filing, Strategy owns eight hundred fifty thousand eight hundred forty-nine bitcoins.
That is about four percent of all bitcoin that will ever exist.
It paid about sixty-four billion dollars for them.
That is an average of about seventy-five thousand dollars per coin.
@scene h6-onderwater
So at sixty thousand dollars, Strategy's bitcoin is worth more than thirteen billion dollars less than it paid.
@scene h6-dats
And Strategy is not alone.
Public companies hold more than one point two million bitcoins, more than six percent of the total supply.
Some of these companies borrowed money to buy them, and they have to pay interest on that debt.
One analyst told CNBC that some of them sold bitcoin to make those payments.
He said these bitcoin treasury companies have made the problem worse.
@scene h6-spacex
Even SpaceX owns bitcoin.
It bought more than eighteen thousand coins, at an average of about thirty-five thousand dollars each.
For SpaceX, it is still a profit.
For many others who bought this year, it is not.
@scene h7-volgende
So what should you watch?
First, the July low, around fifty-seven thousand eight hundred dollars.
Analysts see that as the next big line, and a clear break below it would wipe out the summer recovery.
Second, the bitcoin funds, because the selling only stops when the money stops leaving.
Third, the Fed meeting on December eighth and ninth.
And fourth, companies like Strategy, and whether any of them is forced to sell.
Katie Stockton of Fairlead Strategies said bitcoin, quote, "appears to be trying to find a footing."
But Ryan Kirkley of Global Settlement Network said there is, quote, "a bit more caution than there was a year ago."
None of this is financial advice.
@scene slot
Bitcoin has crashed before, and it has come back before.
But this time, it is falling together with the stock market, not against it.
The question is no longer whether bitcoin is digital gold.
It is who still holds it when the next wave of selling comes.
