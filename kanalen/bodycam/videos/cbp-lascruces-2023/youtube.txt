BESCHRIJVING:
On April 2, 2023, a driver went around a Border Patrol checkpoint on Interstate 10 near Las Cruces, New Mexico. After a 49-minute pursuit, he stepped out of his car holding a wooden club, and agents walked with him through the desert, trying to talk him down.

This documentary uses official body-worn camera footage released by U.S. Customs and Border Protection, with our own narration, editing and research. The moment of the shooting is not shown.
