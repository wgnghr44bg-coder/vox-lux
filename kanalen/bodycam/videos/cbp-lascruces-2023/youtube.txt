BESCHRIJVING:
On April 2, 2023, a driver went around a Border Patrol checkpoint on Interstate 10 near Las Cruces, New Mexico. After a pursuit of almost 50 minutes, he stepped out of his car holding a wooden club, and agents walked with him through the desert, trying to talk him down. This documentary shows what three agents' body cameras recorded that night.

The footage was released by U.S. Customs and Border Protection. We added narration, context and research. The moment of the shooting is blurred, and graphic close-ups have been removed.
