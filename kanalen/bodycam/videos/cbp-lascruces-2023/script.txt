[ORIGINEEL 524.8-535.3 "Drop the stick and we'll talk" ... "Just talk to me."]

The man holding the stick had just led Border Patrol agents on a chase that lasted almost fifty minutes, through the desert west of Las Cruces, New Mexico. Now he was on foot, in the dark. And the agents were trying everything to keep this from ending badly.

It started at eight thirty-seven in the evening, on April second, 2023. A white Nissan Rogue drove around the cones of a Border Patrol checkpoint on Interstate 10, about twenty-three miles west of Las Cruces. Every vehicle has to stop there for inspection. An agent followed the SUV and turned on his lights and siren.

[ORIGINEEL 152.9-181.2 radio: "Just circumvented ... he's motioning the pullover."]

The driver did not stop. He kept going at around forty miles an hour, left the interstate, and turned onto a county road into the desert. After almost eight miles, he reached a closed gate near a ranch. He turned around and stopped. Agents walked up to the SUV with their weapons drawn. They ordered him to show his hands and get out. He did not.

[ORIGINEEL 284.8-295.5 "Open the door" ... "Break it."]

One agent hit the driver's window with his baton, but the glass did not break. The driver pulled away and headed back toward the interstate. At around nine eighteen, agents laid a spike strip across the road. Three of his tires were hit. Still, he kept going, at about twenty-two miles an hour. He drove almost two more miles. Then he turned onto a dirt road, stopped, and got out. In his hand was a wooden club.

[ORIGINEEL 486.2-523.9 "Is there anything I can say to get you to drop the stick?"]

The agents did not rush him. For the next four minutes, they walked with him. They asked his name. They told him they were not there to hurt him.

[ORIGINEEL 555.0-574.4 "Hey, can you talk to me, man?" ... "What's your name?"]
[ORIGINEEL 615.0-635.6 "I don't want to walk out here all night with you" ... "Don't do this."]
[ORIGINEEL 659.6-671.4 "Just talk to me" ... "You got a long walk back home, dude."]

According to Customs and Border Protection, one agent then moved in to try to restrain him. The man came toward the agent, who fell to the ground. The man swung the club and struck the agent on the ground. As he stood over him, ready to strike again, three other agents fired their weapons. About four minutes and twenty seconds had passed since he started walking. We are not showing that moment.

[ORIGINEEL 798.6-811.2 zwart scherm: "I just wish it hadn't ended like this. I tried to talk to him."]

An agent trained as an emergency medical technician checked the driver for a pulse. Other agents called for an ambulance. The sheriff's office and paramedics arrived just after ten that night. Early the next morning, at sixteen minutes past five, the medical investigator declared the driver dead. CBP has only identified him as an adult man, a citizen of the United States. Agents took the wooden club from his hand. And the medical examiner found two closed knives in his pocket.

CBP's Office of Professional Responsibility investigated the shooting, and the case was sent to the U.S. Attorney's Office in New Mexico. The ACLU of New Mexico called for a transparent and independent investigation. CBP said its National Use of Force Review Board would publish its findings. At the time of this video, we could not find those results. What we do have is the footage, and the words of one agent. I tried to talk to him.
