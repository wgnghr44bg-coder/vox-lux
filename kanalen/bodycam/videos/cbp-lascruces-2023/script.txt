[ORIGINEEL 524.8-535.3 cold open "Drop the stick and we'll talk"]

The man holding the stick had just led Border Patrol agents on a chase that lasted almost fifty minutes, through the desert west of Las Cruces, New Mexico. Now he was on foot, in the dark. And the agents were trying everything to keep this from ending badly.

It started at eight thirty-seven in the evening, on April second, 2023. A white Nissan Rogue drove around the cones of a Border Patrol checkpoint on Interstate 10, about twenty-three miles west of Las Cruces. Every vehicle has to stop there for inspection. An agent followed the SUV and turned on his lights and siren.

[ORIGINEEL 138.6-197.5 radio]

The driver did not stop. He kept going at around forty miles an hour, left the interstate, and turned onto a county road into the desert. After almost eight miles, he reached a closed gate near a ranch. He turned around and stopped. Agents walked up to the SUV with their weapons drawn. They ordered him to show his hands and get out. He did not. They tried to open his door, but it would not open. One agent hit the window with his baton, but the glass did not break. Then the driver pulled away again.

[ORIGINEEL 264.0-344.5 bodycam bij het hek + radio]

At around nine eighteen, agents laid a spike strip across the road. Three of his tires were hit. Still, he kept going, at about twenty-two miles an hour. He drove almost two more miles. Then he turned onto a dirt road, stopped, and got out. In his hand was a wooden club. He started walking north, into the dark. The agents followed him on foot.

An agent trained as an emergency medical technician checked the driver for a pulse. Other agents called for an ambulance. The sheriff's office and paramedics arrived just after ten that night. Early the next morning, at sixteen minutes past five, the medical investigator declared the driver dead. CBP has only identified him as an adult man, a citizen of the United States. Agents took the wooden club from his hand. And the medical examiner found two closed knives in his pocket.

CBP's Office of Professional Responsibility investigated the shooting, and the case was sent to the U.S. Attorney's Office in New Mexico. The ACLU of New Mexico called for a transparent and independent investigation. CBP said its National Use of Force Review Board would publish its findings. At the time of this video, we could not find those results. What we do have is the footage, and the words of one agent. I tried to talk to him.
