This is a Border Patrol checkpoint on Interstate 10, west of Las Cruces, New Mexico. On the night of April second, 2023, a white Nissan Rogue drove around it.

[ORIGINEEL 138.6-197.5 radio]

The driver did not stop. Almost eight miles later, at a closed gate near a ranch, he finally did.

[ORIGINEEL 267.2-330.3 bodycam bij het hek + radio] [ORIGINEEL 341.2-345.0 radio spijkermat]

Agents laid a spike strip. Three of his tires were hit, but he kept going. Then he turned onto a dirt road, and got out, holding a wooden club.

[ORIGINEEL 444.3-745.0 het lopen en het schieten (camera 1)]
[ORIGINEEL 798.6-811.2 zwart scherm: "I just wish it hadn't ended like this. I tried to talk to him."]
[ORIGINEEL 850.0-905.5]

This is the same moment, seen by a second agent.

[ORIGINEEL 924.5-948.0 camera 2, schietmoment]

And by a third.

[ORIGINEEL 1088.0-1136.0 camera 3, schietmoment]

The driver died at the scene. CBP has only identified him as an adult man, a citizen of the United States.

The shooting was investigated by CBP, and referred to federal prosecutors. At the time of this video, we could not find the results.
