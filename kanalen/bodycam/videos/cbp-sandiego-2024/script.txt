Just before seven in the morning, high in the mountains east of San Diego, a group of migrants walked along a quiet road, right next to the border with Mexico. They did not know that they were being watched. And they did not know that four men were about to stop them on that road.

[BEELD 1:25-1:58 luchtfoto Otay Mountains]

This is the Otay Mountains area, about five miles east of the Otay Mesa border crossing. A paved road winds through the hills here, close to the border wall. In the weeks before, there had been reports of armed robberies and assaults on migrants crossing into the United States at this spot.

[BEELD 3:40-4:00 + 4:10-4:30 bodycam, agenten verborgen]

So on March third, 2024, a special tactical unit of the Border Patrol, called BORTAC, was hiding in a ravine near the road. On higher ground, about two hundred and seventy-five yards away, a precision marksman was watching the area.

[BEELD 4:30-4:40 de weg]

According to witnesses, four men approached the group of migrants. At least one of them had a handgun. He demanded money, chambered a round, and pointed his pistol at one of the migrants. At that moment, the marksman fired one shot.

[BEELD 6:45-7:05 meldkamer + echte radio "shots fired", zonder stem]

[BEELD 4:55-5:10 agent loopt over de weg]

The armed man fell to the ground. Three other men ran back toward Mexico. What happened in the next hour was recorded by the agents' body cameras, and it shows a side of this story that most people never see.
