On March fourteenth, 2023, a camera near Sasabe, Arizona, about a mile from the Mexican border, saw several people climb into a black BMW. Agents tried to pull it over. The driver did not stop. He turned onto Brown Bear Canyon Road, a rough dirt track into the hills, and tried to turn around.

This is the body camera of the first agent to arrive. For the first two minutes, it recorded no sound. He pulled in behind the BMW, broke two of its windows with his baton, and reached inside, as the car rolled backwards.

There were six other people in the car. One in the front seat, two in the back, and three in the trunk. Agents gave the driver CPR, but he died at the scene. He was thirty-eight years old, a citizen of the United States.

The medical examiner ruled his death a homicide. His family has taken legal action against CBP. At the time of this video, we could not find the outcome.
