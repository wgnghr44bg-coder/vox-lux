This is Sasabe, Arizona, on the border with Mexico. On March fourteenth, 2023, a camera here saw several people get into a black BMW. Agents tried to stop it. The driver kept going, onto a dirt road into the hills.

This is the body camera of the first agent to arrive. It recorded no sound.

He breaks the windows with his baton.

The car is rolling backwards. He reaches inside, and grabs the driver's arm.

Then he fires one shot.

[ORIGINEEL 260.3-395.8 echt geluid]

Six other people were in the car. Three of them were in the trunk. The driver died here. He was thirty-eight years old, a citizen of the United States.

The medical examiner ruled his death a homicide. His family has taken legal action against CBP. At the time of this video, we could not find the outcome.
