Morning. By nine o'clock, every seat is taken. [long pause]
The Sun climbs over the walls, and the stone begins to burn. Thirty degrees. Then thirty-five. [pause]
There is no roof. No shade. Fifty thousand people, packed shoulder to shoulder. [long pause]
Then you notice them, high up on the top of the wall. Sailors. Hundreds of them, sent from the Roman fleet. [pause]
They haul on ropes, tied to two hundred and forty wooden masts. [pause]
And slowly, a giant sail starts to slide out over the crowd. [long pause] [long pause] [long pause] [long pause] [long pause]
The velarium. A roof made of cloth, big enough to shade most of the stands. [long pause]
Down in the arena, the morning shows begin. Hunters, and wild animals from three continents. [pause]
You can't see it from here. But you can hear it. And you can feel fifty thousand people jump to their feet. [long pause]
Noon. The heat is unbearable. You need water, and air, and a way out. [pause]
So you squeeze through the crowd, and take the first staircase you can find. [pause]
But it doesn't lead out. It leads down.
