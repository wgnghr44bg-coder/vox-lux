DAY 1. The army moves in. [long pause]
Tanks, trucks and helicopters close every road within ten kilometres. [pause]
But there is no plan for this. No treaty ever decided what humanity should do if someone simply showed up. [long pause]
So the soldiers get one order. Keep your distance. And do not make the first move. [long pause]
Above them, the ship has been hanging still for twenty-four hours. [pause]
Then dozens of smaller ships break away, and spread out over the city. [long pause]
Beams of light sweep across the water, across the trucks, across every face. [long pause] [long pause] [long pause]
They are not attacking. They are scanning. Every street. Every car. Every one of us. [long pause]
And by the end of the week… they stop watching. And start coming down.
