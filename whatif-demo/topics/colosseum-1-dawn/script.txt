You are standing in a narrow street in Rome. The Sun is not even up yet. [pause]

Not to the market. Not to the temple. [pause]

The Flavian Amphitheatre. Today, we call it the Colosseum. [pause]
Forty-eight metres high. Built in about ten years. Room for around fifty thousand people. [long pause]
At ground level it has eighty arches. Seventy-six of them are numbered, so every visitor can find the way to their seat. [pause]
Someone presses a small clay token into your hand. Gate twenty-three. [pause]
Today, the emperor Titus opens the games. They will go on for one hundred days. [long pause]
