Year one. [long pause]
With no cars and no factories, the air is cleaner than it has been in more than a century. [pause]
Grass pushes up through every crack in the pavement. Each winter, water freezes inside those cracks, and splits them a little wider. [long pause]
Deer wander down streets that were built for cars. [pause]
Foxes raise their young under porches. Birds nest behind broken windows. [long pause]
In the supermarkets, almost every scrap of food has rotted, or been eaten by rats and insects. [long pause]
Year ten. [long pause] [long pause]
Gutters fill with leaves. Rain gets in. Wooden roofs begin to rot, and sag. [pause]
Young trees are already taller than the cars they grow between. [long pause]
