# What if aliens landed tomorrow? – lange video (VOORSTEL, wacht op akkoord eigenaar)

16:9 720p, ± 3:30, filmlook (`post: true`), nieuwe mensen + `groups`, ± 70% wijd / 30% POV.
Stem: atlas, in één keer (one take). Europese eenheden. Speculatief → verteld als would/could; echte feiten waar het kan.
Geen geweld: het leger houdt afstand, niemand raakt gewond.

Teller (boven): **SHIP ALTITUDE** in meters (3.000 m → 2.200 → 900 → 0).
Regel eronder (marker, ook uitgesproken): **HOUR 0 → HOUR 1 → HOUR 3 → HOUR 6 → DAY 1 → WEEK 1**.

## 1. The arrival – HOUR 0 (straat, `street`, middag 15:00) ± 60 s
| shot | soort | beeld |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | WIJD | drukke straat, groepjes mensen, auto's; zonlicht wordt vreemd dof |
| 2 | WIJD | schaduw schuift over de gevels; mensen blijven staan |
| 3 | POV | omhoog kijken: rand van het moederschip schuift voor de zon |
| 4 | WIJD | hoog: moederschip (± 3 km) boven de hele stad |
| 5 | WIJD | stille climax: auto's stoppen, iedereen kijkt omhoog, schip hangt stil |
| 6 | POV | groepje met telefoons omhoog |

VO:
- Imagine aliens landed tomorrow.
- Not a signal from a distant star. Not a blurry light in the night sky. Just… there. Hanging over your city.
- It is three in the afternoon. The street is busy. Then the sunlight turns strangely dim.
- People stop walking. Something is sliding in front of the Sun.
- It is almost three kilometres wide. Wider than the whole city centre.
- The nearest star is more than four light-years away. Our fastest spacecraft would need over six thousand years to get there.
- Whoever built this did not just cross that distance. They arrived.
- And now… it is not moving. **[3 s stil]**
- But here's the terrifying part. Our radio and TV signals have been leaking into space for about a hundred years.
- This may not be an accident. They could have been listening. *(cliffhanger)*

## 2. Breaking news – HOUR 1 → HOUR 6 (NIEUW: tv-studio + huiskamer + etalage) ± 55 s
| shot | soort | beeld |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | WIJD | tv-studio: nieuwslezer, schermen achter met het schip, BREAKING NEWS-balk |
| 2 | WIJD | huiskamer: gezin op de bank, tv met hetzelfde beeld, schip zichtbaar door het raam |
| 3 | WIJD | etalage met rij tv's, groepje mensen ervoor (straat) |
| 4 | POV | kijken naar telefoon/tv: "NO NETWORK" |
| 5 | WIJD | studio: koersen rood, "TRADING HALTED" op het scherm |
| 6 | WIJD | stille climax in de huiskamer: alle schermen worden zwart |

VO:
- HOUR 1. Every channel on Earth shows the same picture.
- More people are watching than the six hundred million who saw the first Moon landing.
- Then the phone networks fail. Not because of an attack. Just because everyone calls the people they love, at the same time.
- HOUR 3. Stock markets stop trading. Automatic brakes kick in when prices fall too fast.
- Supermarket shelves empty. We saw it happen in 2020, over weeks. Now it takes one afternoon.
- HOUR 6. The ship has still done nothing. No message. No sound.
- Then every screen goes black. **[3 s stil]**
- And when the picture comes back… the ship is lower. *(cliffhanger)*

## 3. The army – DAY 1 (rivierstad, `river-city`, ochtend) ± 55 s
| shot | soort | beeld |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | WIJD | colonne legertrucks en tanks over de kade |
| 2 | WIJD | wegversperring vóór de brug, soldaten op afstand (brug blijft heel) |
| 3 | WIJD | helikopters cirkelen; moederschip lager (2.200 m) boven de rivier |
| 4 | POV | soldaat bij de versperring kijkt omhoog |
| 5 | WIJD | tientallen kleine schepen maken zich los en waaieren uit |
| 6 | WIJD | stille climax: lichtbundels glijden over rivier, trucks en soldaten |

VO:
- DAY 1. The army moves in.
- Tanks, trucks and helicopters close every road within ten kilometres.
- But there is no plan for this. No treaty ever decided what humanity should do if someone simply showed up.
- So the soldiers get one order. Keep your distance. And do not make the first move.
- Above them, the ship has been hanging still for twenty-four hours.
- Then dozens of smaller ships break away, and spread out over the city.
- Beams of light sweep across the water, across the trucks, across every face. **[3 s stil]**
- They are not attacking. They are scanning. Every street. Every car. Every one of us.
- And by the end of the week… they stop watching. And start coming down. *(cliffhanger)*

## 4. Contact – WEEK 1 (strandboulevard, `boulevard`, zonsopkomst) ± 60 s
| shot | soort | beeld |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | WIJD | strand bij dageraad, één schip daalt langzaam (900 m) |
| 2 | WIJD | rij soldaten op 500 m, daarachter een grote menigte (groups) |
| 3 | POV | tussen de menigte door naar het schip |
| 4 | WIJD | zand waait op, motoren worden stil, schip zweeft laag |
| 5 | WIJD | STILLE CLIMAX (± 5 s): schip zet neer (0 m), deur gaat open, licht stroomt naar buiten |
| 6 | WIJD | hoog: moederschip boven de baai bij zonsopkomst, uitvloeien naar zwart |

VO:
- WEEK 1. Dawn. Over the beach, a single ship begins to descend.
- Anyone who can travel between stars would be far ahead of us. Not hundreds of years. Perhaps millions.
- The soldiers form a line, five hundred metres back. Behind them, thousands of people who refused to stay home.
- The engines fall silent. The sand stops moving. **[5 s stil]**
- A door opens. Light pours out onto the sand.
- And for the first time in history, we know for certain… we are not alone.
- So tell me. If they stepped out tomorrow… would you walk out to meet them, or lock your door?
