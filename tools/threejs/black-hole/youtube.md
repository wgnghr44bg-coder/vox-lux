## Beschrijving
Imagine waking up tomorrow… and discovering that the stars are moving.

Distant stars bend into strange arcs. One star splits into several images. Another vanishes.
Something invisible is warping the light from entire regions of the galaxy — a black hole is heading
toward our solar system, and nobody knows how close it will come.

A black hole can't be seen directly. You only see what its gravity does: bent starlight, ghostly
Einstein rings, distorted stars. This cinematic 3D science thriller follows astronomers from the
first warning to the closest approach — and what the encounter could do to Earth's orbit.

The scenario is hypothetical; the physics (gravitational lensing, orbits bound to the Sun, the
estimated ~100 million invisible black holes in our galaxy) is real.
