Imagine if it never stopped raining.

{img:c02}
Not for a day. Not for a week. Not even for a year.

{img:c03}
At first, it would be nothing more than an inconvenience.

{img:c04}
But as days turn into weeks, the ground would become saturated.

{img:c05}
Rivers would overflow. Drains would fail.

{img:c06}
Water would pour into streets, gardens and homes.

{img:c07}
Within months, power would fail and cars would float away.

{img:c08}
Underground, the drains would be completely overwhelmed.

{img:c09}
After a year, roads would become rivers and cars would become wrecks…

{img:c10}
…and rooftops would become islands.

{img:c11}
Ten years later, the world we knew might be almost unrecognizable.

{img:c12}
But the rain would still be falling.
