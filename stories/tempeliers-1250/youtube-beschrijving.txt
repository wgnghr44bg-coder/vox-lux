Drift off to the story of life among the Knights Templar in 1250, seen through the eyes of a young knight from Champagne who joins the order. A calm history for sleep about the real Templars: monks and knights, farmers and bankers, from a quiet commandery in France to the crusader port of Acre.
Kneel in the candlelit chapel for the reception, learn the Rule and the hours of prayer, share a bowl at the silent table, care for the great war horses, visit the treasury of the Temple in Paris, sail across the Mediterranean, walk through the markets of Acre, live in the Pilgrims' Castle by the sea, ride with pilgrims to Nazareth, and come home at last as the commander of the house.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
