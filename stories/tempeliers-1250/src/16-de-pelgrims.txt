The Templars were founded to protect pilgrims. And even in 1250, after Jerusalem has been lost, pilgrims still come to the Holy Land. They visit the holy places that are still in Christian hands, or that they are allowed to visit under peace agreements. And the Templars still ride beside them, to keep them safe on the roads.

--

Every spring, the ships from Europe bring hundreds of pilgrims to Acre. Men and women, rich and poor, young and old. Some come to pray for a loved one. Some come to ask forgiveness for their sins. Some come because they have made a promise to God. Some come simply because they have always dreamed of seeing the places where Jesus lived. For many of them, it is the greatest journey of their lives.

From Acre, the pilgrims travel to the holy places in groups. One of the most popular journeys is to Nazareth, in the hills of Galilee, where Jesus grew up. It is about a day's ride from Acre. And the Templars often provide an escort. A group of knights and sergeants rides with the pilgrims, at the front and at the back, watching the road.

{img:nazareth-hills-village-church-pilgrims-arriving-evening-light}
Guillaume rides with such a group, one spring. There are about fifty pilgrims, from France, Germany and England. Among them is Jan, the old merchant from Flanders, whom Guillaume met on the ship. They are happy to see each other again. They ride side by side, and talk about everything that has happened since the voyage. Jan says that Guillaume looks much older now, and browner from the sun.

The road winds up into the hills, through olive groves and little villages. The spring flowers are blooming everywhere. Red anemones, yellow daisies, purple irises. The air smells of herbs and earth. The pilgrims sing hymns as they walk. Some walk barefoot, as a sign of humility. In the evening, they reach Nazareth, a small town on a hillside, with a big church built over the place where, they believe, the angel came to Mary.

--

{img:pilgrims-praying-in-church-nazareth-candles-kneeling-tears}
In the church, the pilgrims kneel and pray. Many of them cry. They have travelled thousands of kilometres, for months, to be here. Jan kneels in a corner, and prays for his wife for a long time. Guillaume kneels beside him. He prays for his family, for the brothers at home, for old Brother Raoul. And he prays that he may be a good Templar, and a good man.

The pilgrims also visit the Sea of Galilee, a beautiful lake surrounded by hills, where Jesus once walked by the shore and talked to the fishermen. The water is calm and blue. Fishermen are still fishing there, in little boats, just as in the time of the Gospels. The pilgrims fill little bottles with water from the lake, to take home. Some of them wade into the water, and wash their faces.

{img:sea-of-galilee-calm-blue-lake-fishing-boats-hills-pilgrims-shore}
Many pilgrims collect souvenirs. Little bottles of holy water or oil. Small badges made of lead, with a picture of the holy place, which they sew onto their hats. Palm leaves from Jericho. Stones and pebbles from the holy places. When they go home, they will show these things to their families and friends, and tell the story of their journey for the rest of their lives.

--

On the way back to Acre, Jan rides beside Guillaume in silence for a long time. Then he says that this will be his last pilgrimage. He is getting old. He will go home to Flanders, to his children and grandchildren, and stay there. He thanks Guillaume for keeping them safe. At the harbour of Acre, they say goodbye. Jan gives Guillaume a little lead badge, with a picture of the church of Nazareth. Guillaume asks the commander for permission to keep it. And this time too, the answer is yes.

At night, on the road, the pilgrims sleep in a guesthouse in a little village, or in tents. The Templars take turns standing guard, while the others sleep. Guillaume stands under the stars, with his cloak around him, listening to the crickets and the soft breathing of the sleeping pilgrims. The hills are silver in the moonlight. And everything is very quiet and very peaceful.
