Drift off to the story of life in Edo, the great city of Japan that we now call Tokyo, around the year 1800. A calm history for sleep about lantern makers, public baths, cherry blossoms and quiet evenings by the river.
Meet a family of paper lantern makers, follow them to the fish market, the public bath and the little neighbourhood school, walk the old Tokaido road, picnic under the cherry blossoms, watch the summer fireworks over the Sumida river, visit the kabuki theatre and the sumo wrestlers, and welcome the New Year with the first sunrise.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
