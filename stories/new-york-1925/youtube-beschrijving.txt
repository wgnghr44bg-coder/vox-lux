Drift off to the calm, true story of life in New York City in 1925, in the middle of Prohibition and the Jazz Age.
Slip through the hidden doors of the speakeasies, ride the nickel subway, hear jazz in Harlem, eat at the automat, walk among the first great skyscrapers and spend a summer day at Coney Island.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
