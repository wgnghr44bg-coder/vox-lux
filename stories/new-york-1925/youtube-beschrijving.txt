Drift off to the story of life in New York in 1925, in the middle of the Roaring Twenties, seen through the eyes of an Italian-American family in Little Italy. A calm history for sleep about taxis, radio, jazz and a city full of dreams.
Ride with a taxi driver through the busy streets, sit with a seamstress in the garment district, sell newspapers on the corner, gather around the new radio in the kitchen, hear the gospel choirs of Harlem, watch Babe Ruth hit a home run, spend a summer day at Coney Island, follow the brave sled dogs of Nome, and see the city in the snow at Christmas.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
