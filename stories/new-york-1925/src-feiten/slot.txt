So the long night of 1925 grows quiet over New York.

The last taxis roll home along the empty avenues. In a basement on a side street, a band packs up its instruments, and the small hidden door closes softly. Up on the rooftops, the old wooden water towers stand dark against the sky, and far below, the river carries the reflections of a thousand windows out towards the sea.

Not one of these names or numbers has to stay with you. The city can keep its secrets, and the music can fade into the hum of the streets.

Before the morning comes, another unhurried story of a faraway city waits patiently on the screen. Subscribing keeps the door open for the next one.

Sleep well, and good night.
