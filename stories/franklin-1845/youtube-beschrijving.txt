Drift off to the story of Sir John Franklin's lost expedition of 1845, two ships and a hundred and twenty-nine men who sailed into the Arctic ice to find the Northwest Passage. A calm history for sleep.
Sail with Erebus and Terror from the Thames to Greenland, spend the long polar nights on board, meet the Netsilik Inuit, follow the last journey gently and from a distance, and see how Lady Franklin, John Rae and the Inuit kept searching until the ships were found again.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
