Drift off to the calm, true story of life aboard Erebus and Terror, the two ships of Sir John Franklin's lost Arctic expedition of 1845.
Spend the long polar night below deck with the sailors, meet the Inuit of the far north, follow the search that lasted more than a decade, and see how the lost ships were finally found beneath the ice in 2014 and 2016.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
