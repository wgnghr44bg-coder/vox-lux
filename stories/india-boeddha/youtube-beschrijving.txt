Drift off to the story of ancient India around 500 BC, on the green plain of the river Ganges, in the time of the Buddha. A calm history for sleep.
Walk through the villages and rice fields, wait for the monsoon, visit the cities of Rajagriha, Shravasti and Varanasi, travel with the merchants' caravans, sit with the forest sages, follow Prince Siddhartha from his birth in a garden to the night under the Bodhi tree, spend a rainy season with the monks, listen to the old stories of the monkey king and the hare in the moon, and visit the places of the Buddha's life as they are today. Told gently and respectfully, simply to look and listen.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
