For forty-five years, the Buddha walked and taught across the Ganges plain.

When he was eighty years old, he was tired and frail. He set out on one last journey to the north. His faithful helper Ananda went with him, and a group of monks.

--

They walked slowly from village to village. They crossed the Ganges by boat. They stopped at a city where he stayed in a mango grove for the last rainy season. There he fell ill. Everyone was worried. But he got better, and went on.

He stood on a hill outside the city and looked back at it for a long time. How lovely this city is, Ananda, he said. This is the last time I will look at it.

At last they came to a small town and a grove of trees outside it. There the Buddha lay down between two great trees. He lay on his right side with his head resting on his hand. He was calm and peaceful.

--

The stories say that even though it was not the season, the trees burst into blossom. And the flowers fell softly on him, like rain.

Ananda was very sad. He went away and leaned against a doorpost and cried. The Buddha called for him. Do not be sad, Ananda, he said gently. Have I not told you that everything we love must change and pass away? You have looked after me so well and so kindly for so many years. Keep going. And you too will soon be free.

{img:monks-gathered-quietly-sal-grove-night-lamps-reverent-stillness}
He asked the monks three times if any of them had a question he could still answer, while there was time. They were all silent.

--

Then he spoke his last words. All things that are made will pass away. Keep going, with care.

And then, quietly and peacefully, in the night under the flowering trees, he passed away. Buddhists call it the final peace.

We will leave him there, gently, under the blossoming trees in the quiet grove. The monks sit in silence around him. The lamps burn. And the night is soft and still.

{img:parinirvana-stupa-kushinagar-today-white-dome-pilgrims-flowers}
Today at that place there is a white dome and a temple. Inside is a great statue of the Buddha lying peacefully on his right side. Pilgrims bring golden cloth and lay it over him. And the same kind of trees still grow in the park around it. Every spring, they blossom.
