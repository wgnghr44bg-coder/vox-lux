Tonight we are going about two and a half thousand years back, to the great plain of the river Ganges, in northern India, around the year 500 BC.

--

It is early morning. The sun is rising, red, and huge, through the mist, over a wide, slow river, and its light spreads, across the water, in a long, golden path. On the banks, there are villages, among mango trees, and palms, and the green fields of rice, and the smoke of the morning fires is rising, into the still air. A boat is crossing the river, slowly, the boatman pushing it along, with a long pole. And on the steps, down to the water, people are bathing, and praying, facing the rising sun.

Somewhere, a bird is calling, a koel, its clear, rising call, over and over, from the trees.

If you enjoy calm journeys into the past like this one, you are warmly welcome to subscribe to the channel, so you can find the next one easily. And now, let us begin.

--

This is India, in the time of the Buddha.

{img:indian-village-500bc-thatched-huts-banyan-tree-cattle-morning-women-water}
It is a time of great change. All across the plain of the Ganges, forests are being cleared, and new fields planted, and villages are growing into towns, and towns into cities, with walls, and gates, and markets, and palaces. New kingdoms are rising, and old ones are falling. Merchants travel, with their caravans, from city to city, and the first coins are being made.

And it is a time of great questions. Many people, rich, and poor, kings, and merchants, and farmers, are asking: What is the meaning of life? Why is there suffering? What happens after death? How can we find peace? And some of them leave their homes, and their families, and go out, into the forests, to look for the answers.

--

One of them is a young prince, from a small country, at the foot of the Himalayas. His name is Siddhartha Gautama. And after many years of searching, he will find an answer, sitting, one night, under a great tree, by a river, and he will become known, as the Buddha, the one who has woken up.

Tonight, we are going to travel through the India of his time. We will walk through the villages, and the rice fields, and the great cities, of Rajagriha, and Shravasti, and Varanasi. We will sit with the farmers, and the merchants, and the kings, and the forest sages. We will watch the monsoon come, and spend the rainy season, in a quiet grove. And we will follow the Buddha, gently, from his birth, in a garden, to his last journey.

{img:bodhi-tree-pipal-leaves-heart-shaped-sunlight-quiet-grove}
We will not try to teach, or to persuade, but simply to look, and listen, quietly, at a world, long ago, and the people who lived in it.

--

So let us walk down to the river, in the early morning, with the mist on the water, and the sound of the koel, and the bells of the cattle, and the smell of woodsmoke, and wet earth, and begin.
