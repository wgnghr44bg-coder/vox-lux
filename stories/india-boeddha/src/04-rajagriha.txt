Let us visit Rajagriha, the capital of Magadha.

It lies in a valley with five rocky hills all around it, covered with forest. The hills are like the walls of a great fortress. And along the tops of the hills there are real walls too. They are made of great rough stones piled up for kilometres around the whole valley. Gates stand in the gaps between the hills. Parts of these walls are still there today among the trees. They are among the oldest stone walls in India.

--

Inside is the city. There is the king's palace. There are houses of nobles and traders and craftsmen. They are built of wood and brick and clay, with straw or tile roofs. They have balconies and courtyards and gardens. There are markets and streets full of people and ox carts and elephants and horses.

Around the valley, among the hills, there are hot springs. People come to bathe in the warm water. There are caves in the hills where hermits live and sit in silence. And on one hill there is a rocky peak shaped like a vulture. Later the Buddha will often stay there and teach. From the top he can look out over the valley and the plains.

{img:vulture-peak-rajgir-rocky-summit-sunset-plains-below-quiet}
Outside the city walls there is a beautiful bamboo grove. It is cool and green and rustles in the wind. There is a pond where squirrels play. The king will give this grove to the Buddha and his followers as a place to stay. It will be the first of the great monasteries.

--

There is also a mango grove. It belongs to the king's doctor, Jivaka. He is the most famous doctor of his time. He studied medicine for seven years in a great city far to the north-west. He looks after the king and the Buddha and his followers. And he looks after the poor for free.

There is a story about his last test at school. His teacher sent him into the forest. Find a plant that has no use as medicine, the teacher said. Jivaka searched for a long time. Days went by. Then he came back. Teacher, he said, I have not found a single plant that cannot be used as medicine. And the teacher smiled. Then you are ready, he said.

{img:physician-jivaka-mango-grove-preparing-herbal-medicine-mortar-patients}
Rajagriha is a busy rich noisy city. Traders and soldiers and officials come and go all day long. But it is also a place where wise men and seekers come, to the caves and groves around the hills. King Bimbisara likes to visit them and listen to them and ask them questions.

--

Today the place is a small town in the north-east of India. It is still ringed by its five hills. The remains of the old walls are still on their slopes. The hot springs still flow. And visitors come from all over the world to climb the vulture peak at sunrise or sunset. They sit quietly where the Buddha sat and look out over the green valley and the plains.
