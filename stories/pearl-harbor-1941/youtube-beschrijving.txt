Drift off to the story of Pearl Harbor and the island of Oahu, Hawaii, in December 1941, in the last calm weeks before the attack that brought America into the Second World War. A calm history for sleep.
Spend easy days in old Honolulu and on Waikiki Beach, live on board the battleships with the young sailors and with Dorie Miller, listen to the bands at the Battle of Music on the last Saturday night, follow that Sunday morning gently and from a distance, and stand on the white memorial over the USS Arizona as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
