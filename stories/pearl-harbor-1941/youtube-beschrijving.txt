Drift off to the story of Pearl Harbor and the island of Oahu in the autumn of 1941, and what everyday life was really like there before that Sunday morning in December. A calm history for sleep.
Walk through Honolulu and Waikiki, spend slow days aboard a battleship with the young sailors and the ship's band, meet Dorie Miller, a farm boy from Texas, and follow the last Saturday and the Sunday dawn gently and from a distance, through the courage and kindness that followed, to the quiet white memorial on the harbour today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
