And here, on the quiet water, our visit to the island comes to its end.

The sun has slipped behind the western mountains. The boats have stopped running, and the white memorial stands alone on the harbour. A bugle plays softly as the flag comes down for the night. On Ford Island, the palms grow dark against a sky of rose and gold. Then the first stars appear, one by one. Little waves lap against the white walls, above the old ship that sleeps below.

Still awake? Then let another calm story keep you company. You'll find it waiting on the screen. And if this evening helped you rest, subscribing will bring the next one to you.

The trade wind is soft now, and the night is warm. Your day can rest here, beside the harbour, like a boat tied up for the night.

Sleep well, and good night.
