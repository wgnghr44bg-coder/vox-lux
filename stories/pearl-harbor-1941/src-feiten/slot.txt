We have seen Honolulu on Boat Day, the battleships at their moorings, the sailors on liberty, and the long morning that changed the island. We have seen how people helped one another, and how the harbour was slowly rebuilt.

Now the light fades over the water. The white memorial stands alone, and the palms on Ford Island turn dark. Nothing here needs to be held on to.

Not drifting off yet? Another unhurried story, about a different time and place, sits ready on the screen. A subscription keeps the next one within reach.

Breathe slowly, and let the warm island night settle around you.

Sleep well, and good night.
