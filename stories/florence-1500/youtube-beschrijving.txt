Drift off to the story of life in Renaissance Florence around the year 1500, the city of the great dome, of Leonardo da Vinci and the young Michelangelo. A calm history for sleep about craftsmen, artists and a marble giant.
Meet a family of a stonemason and a silk weaver, visit the markets and the painters' workshops, watch Leonardo paint a quiet young woman with a gentle smile, see Michelangelo carve David from an old block of marble, follow the four slow days when the giant was carried through the streets, and celebrate the feast of San Giovanni with fireworks over the Arno.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
