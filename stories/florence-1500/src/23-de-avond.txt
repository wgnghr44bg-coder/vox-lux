So we leave Florence tonight, around the year 1500, as the evening falls.

The sun is setting behind the hills to the west. The sky turns gold, then pink, then a deep soft blue. The great dome of the cathedral glows red and gold above the rooftops. The river Arno shines like a ribbon of light, flowing slowly under its bridges. Swallows dive and swoop over the roofs. And the bells of the city ring for evening prayers, one after another, from every church tower.

--

In the main square, the people are walking slowly in the evening air. They stop to look up at David, standing white and tall in front of the old palace. He is glowing softly in the last light of the sun. Pigeons settle on his shoulders and his head. And he goes on looking out across the square, calm and patient, as if he is waiting for something.

{img:florence-house-evening-family-loom-lamp-grandmother-children-drawing}
In the family's house in the narrow street, the lamps are being lit. Caterina is finishing the last lines of her silk for the day. Piero is sitting by the fire, with his tired hands in his lap. Matteo is at the table, with his drawings spread out in front of him. He is still working on his drawing of David, adding little details by the light of the lamp.

--

Up on the loggia, Bianca is sitting beside Nonna Lucia, wrapped in a shawl. They are watching the sky over the rooftops. The first stars are coming out above the great dome. Nonna is humming the old carnival song of Lorenzo the Magnificent, very softly. How beautiful is youth, which flies away so fast. Bianca leans her head on her grandmother's shoulder and listens.

{img:girl-and-grandmother-rooftop-loggia-dusk-stars-dome-humming-calm}
Bianca thinks about all the things she has seen. The great dome and the golden doors. The silk on her mother's loom. The painters' workshop and the old storyteller. The fireworks over the river, and the new map of the world. And the giant marble shepherd, carried through the streets, with his calm and serious face. She thinks she is very lucky, to live in such a city, at such a time.

--

Below them, the street grows quiet. The shoemaker has closed his shop. A cat walks slowly along a wall. Somewhere, someone is playing a lute, a soft gentle tune. A watchman walks past with a lantern, calling out the hour. And above the red roofs of Florence, the stars come out, more and more, until the whole sky is shining.
