Drift off to the story of Easter Island, Rapa Nui, the tiny island in the middle of the Pacific Ocean with its giant stone faces. A calm history for sleep about the moai, the people who made them, and the mysteries that remain.
Sail with the Polynesian navigators who found the island by the stars, meet a family of moai carvers around the year 1500, watch the statues being carved at Rano Raraku and walking to the sea, see the great race of the birdman, wonder at the rongorongo writing that nobody can read, meet the Dutch sailors of Jacob Roggeveen on Easter Sunday 1722, and see how the people of Rapa Nui keep their culture alive today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
