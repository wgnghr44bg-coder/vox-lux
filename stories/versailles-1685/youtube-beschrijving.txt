Drift off to a summer at the Palace of Versailles in 1685, in the golden age of King Louis XIV, the Sun King. A calm history for sleep about the palace, its famous gardens and fountains, and the gardener's family who helped to keep it all so beautiful.
Pick figs and early peas in the king's kitchen garden, watch the morning ceremony of the king's rising, walk through the brand-new Hall of Mirrors, run with the fountain boys and their whistles, glide on the Grand Canal in a Venetian gondola, breathe in the orange blossom of the Orangery, carry flowers by lantern light at the Trianon, and watch the fireworks over the water on a summer night.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
