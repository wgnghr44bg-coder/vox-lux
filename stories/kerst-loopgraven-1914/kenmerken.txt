# trefwoord | hoe het er in december 1914 echt uitzag
trench | a muddy zigzag trench dug into flat Flanders farmland, walls held up with sandbags and wooden planks, wooden duckboards on the floor, barbed wire on wooden stakes in front, no concrete
soldiers | British soldiers in 1914 wear khaki wool uniforms and soft flat peaked caps, no steel helmets; German soldiers wear grey-green uniforms and spiked Pickelhaube helmets with cloth covers or soft round caps
