On a winter evening in Flanders, the sky is very wide, and the light fades early. The sun goes down behind the poplar trees, red and golden, and the frost begins to settle on the fields. The lights come on in the farmhouses. A dog barks in a farmyard. The church bell rings for the evening. You'd stand very still, with everyone else, until the last note fades. And over the flat, quiet land, the first stars come out, one by one, as they did on that Christmas Eve, long ago.
