And so we leave Flanders, under a sky full of winter stars.

The frost glitters on the flat fields, where poplar trees stand in long, quiet rows. In the farmhouses, the lamps go out one by one, and the little church spires rise dark against the night. Somewhere, very faintly, it's almost as if you can still hear a carol drifting across the fields, in two languages at once.

Eyes still open? Then another story, from another winter in history, waits patiently for you, there on the screen. And if this Christmas tale gave you some calm, a subscription will bring you the next.

Let the silent night settle softly around you. All is calm now.

Sleep well, and good night.
