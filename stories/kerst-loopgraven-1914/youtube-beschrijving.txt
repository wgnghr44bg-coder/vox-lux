Drift off to the story of the Christmas truce of 1914, when British and German soldiers in the trenches of Flanders laid down their rifles and met in No Man's Land. A calm history for sleep.
Follow young soldiers from the golden summer of 1914 to the muddy trenches near Plugstreet Wood, share their tea, their letters and their parcels from home, listen to the carols drifting across the frozen fields on Christmas Eve, and walk through the quiet fields of Flanders as they are today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
