Drift off to the story of life aboard a whaling ship in 1850, seen through the eyes of a fourteen-year-old farm boy who sails from New Bedford for three years at sea. A calm history for sleep about the ship, the crew, the wide oceans and the long way home.
Climb to the lookout at the top of the mast, carve scrimshaw by lamplight, sail around stormy Cape Horn, post a letter in the old barrel on the Galapagos, visit another ship in the middle of the Pacific, rest in Lahaina on Hawaii, find a way through the Arctic ice, and come home at last to the apple trees in blossom.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
