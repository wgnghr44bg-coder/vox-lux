Drift off to the story of life in London under Queen Elizabeth I in 1599, the year the Globe Theatre was built, seen through the eyes of a glover's family in Cheapside. A calm history for sleep about a crowded, lively city on the Thames, in the golden age of England.
Open the shop shutters on Cheapside, walk through the markets with a basket, cross the river with the watermen, see the queen's golden barge glide past, walk across London Bridge with its houses, listen to the gossip in St Paul's, spin the globe at the Royal Exchange, stand in the new Globe to watch Julius Caesar, sing madrigals by candlelight, and watch the knights at the queen's tournament.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
