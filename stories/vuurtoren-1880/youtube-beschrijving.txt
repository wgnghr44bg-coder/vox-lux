Drift off to the life of a lighthouse keeper in 1880, on a small rocky island off the west coast of Scotland, with a keeper's family who keep the light burning every night from sunset to sunrise. A calm history for sleep about the lamp and the great glass lens, the long night watches, the sea birds and seals, and a quiet life surrounded by the sea.
Climb the spiral stairs at sunset to light the lamp, wind the clockwork through the night, polish the brass until it shines, read the weather from the sky, wait for the supply boat and the mail bag, watch the puffins on the cliffs, ring the fog bell through a misty night, keep the light burning through an Atlantic storm, and celebrate Hogmanay with the fiddle while the beam sweeps over the sea.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
