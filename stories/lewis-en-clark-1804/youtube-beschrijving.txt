Drift off to the story of Lewis and Clark and the Corps of Discovery, who set out up the Missouri River in 1804 to cross an unknown continent to the Pacific. A calm history for sleep.
Travel slowly with the keelboat and the canoes, spend a long winter with the Mandan, meet Sacagawea and her baby, cross the Rocky Mountains with the Shoshone and the Nez Perce, and follow the great rivers down to the ocean, then home again, and along the trail as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
