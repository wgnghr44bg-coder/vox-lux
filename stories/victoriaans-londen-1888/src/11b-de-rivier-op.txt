On a summer day, many Londoners go up the river.

Little steamboats leave from the piers in the middle of town every few minutes. They are crowded with families and couples and children. Everyone wears summer clothes and straw hats. The boats go up the river, away from the city, toward the green countryside.

--

As the boat goes upriver, the city slowly falls behind. The great buildings and factories give way to houses with gardens. The gardens run down to the water. There are boathouses and willow trees and meadows. The river grows narrower and quieter and greener.

At Kew there are the royal gardens. There are wide lawns and great trees from all over the world. There are flower beds and lakes. And there are huge glass houses of iron and glass. Inside the biggest one it is warm and steamy and full of tall palm trees. And a Chinese tower rises above the trees.

{img:kew-gardens-palm-house-glasshouse-victorian-visitors-lawn-summer}
At Richmond there is a hill with a famous view over the bend of the river. Painters have painted it for a hundred years. There are boats to hire. Young men in striped jackets and flat straw hats row on the river. Young ladies in white sit with parasols. They trail their fingers in the cool water.

--

This is the great age of boating on the Thames. Every summer weekend the river is crowded. There are rowing boats and flat boats pushed with long poles. There are canoes and little steam launches. Houseboats are tied up along the banks, with flower boxes on their roofs.

Next year, a writer called Jerome K. Jerome will publish a funny book. It is about three young men and a dog on a boat trip up the Thames. It is called Three Men in a Boat. Things keep going wrong for them, one after another. It will become one of the best-loved funny books in English. People still read it today.

{img:rowing-boats-punts-river-thames-willows-summer-afternoon-picnic}
Further up is Hampton Court, the great old palace of King Henry the Eighth. It has red brick and tall chimneys and gardens. And it has a famous maze of hedges. Visitors get lost in it. They laugh and try to find the way out.

--

And in the evening, as the sun sets, the steamboats carry the tired happy families back down the river. They glide through the golden light, past the willows and the meadows. The children fall asleep on their mothers' laps. And ahead of them, in the dusk, the lights of London begin to shine.
