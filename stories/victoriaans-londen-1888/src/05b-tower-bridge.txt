Beside the Tower of London, in 1888, something new is rising from the river.

Two huge bases of granite and concrete have been sunk into the bottom of the Thames. On top of them, workers are building two tall towers of steel. It is a new bridge. It will be called Tower Bridge.

--

For years, the people of the East End have needed a new bridge here. But there is a problem. A bridge must not stop the tall ships. They need to sail up the river to the quays beside the Tower.

So the builders thought of something clever. The middle of the bridge will have two great arms. They can be lifted up, like a drawbridge, to let tall ships pass through. Then they can be lowered again for the traffic.

{img:workers-riveting-steel-high-tower-bridge-river-below-boats}
The building began two years ago. It will take eight years. Hundreds of men are working on it. They join the steel together high above the river, with hot metal pins. There are no safety nets. One wrong step, and they would fall into the water far below. So they move slowly and carefully, every day.

--

The towers are made of steel to make them strong. But they will be covered with stone. That way they will look like old towers, to match the Tower of London beside them. High up between the towers there will be two walkways. People can cross there even when the bridge is open.

The great arms will be lifted by steam engines hidden inside the bases. They will rise smoothly and quickly, in about a minute.

--

When it is finished, the prince will open it. And it will become the most famous bridge in the world, the symbol of London.

{img:tower-of-london-white-tower-walls-river-thames-autumn-afternoon}
Beside it stands the Tower of London. It is already eight hundred years old. It has watched over everything since William the Conqueror built it. The ravens hop about on the grass. The guards in their dark blue uniforms show visitors around and tell them the old stories. And the crown jewels glitter behind their railings.

--

In 1888, many Londoners come to watch the new bridge being built. They stand on the river bank and look at the great steel towers. Every month they rise a little higher. Will it really open in the middle? Will it work? People wonder what it will look like when it is done.

Today, more than a hundred and thirty years later, Tower Bridge still opens several hundred times a year. It opens for tall ships and sailing boats. The traffic stops. People stop and watch. And the great arms rise slowly into the sky.
