Drift off to the story of Victorian London in 1888, the greatest city in the world, with its gaslit streets, horse omnibuses and hansom cabs, its fog and its river. A calm history for sleep.
Ride on the top of an omnibus and on the steam trains of the first underground railway, visit a family home in Kensington and spend a day with a young housemaid, walk through Covent Garden and Billingsgate, watch Tower Bridge being built, spend an evening at the music hall and the Savoy Theatre, walk through the fog with Sherlock Holmes, follow the lamplighter at dusk, and visit Victorian London as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
