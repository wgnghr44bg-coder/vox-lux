Drift off to the calm, true history of the Dancing Plague of Strasbourg, the summer of 1518 when hundreds of people danced in the streets and could not stop.
Walk through the timber-framed streets of a free city on the Rhine, its pink sandstone cathedral, guild halls and markets, the hungry years before 1518, the doctors and the city council, the musicians hired to play, the pilgrimage to the shrine of Saint Vitus and what historians now believe caused it.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
