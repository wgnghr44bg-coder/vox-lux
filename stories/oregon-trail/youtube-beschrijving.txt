Drift off to the story of the Oregon Trail, the long road west that carried families in covered wagons from Independence, Missouri, across the prairie and the mountains to the green Willamette Valley in the spring, summer and autumn of 1846. A calm history for sleep.
Walk beside the oxen through the long days on the Platte, sit by the campfires at night, pass Chimney Rock, Independence Rock and South Pass, and follow Tabitha Brown and the other travellers to their new homes in Oregon, gently and without frightening details.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
and softly changing images.
