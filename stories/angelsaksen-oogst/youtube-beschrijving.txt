Drift off to the story of harvest time in an Anglo-Saxon village in the year 700, in the kingdom of Wessex in the south of England. A calm history for sleep about one farming family, their golden fields and the most important weeks of their year.
Bake the first loaf of the new harvest for Lammas, cut the wheat with sickles under the August sun, bring in the sheaves before a summer storm, braid the last sheaf into a corn dolly, travel to the market by the sea, listen to the poet in the lord's hall, look at the beautiful books of the monks, and celebrate harvest home around the bonfire.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
