Drift off to the legend of King Arthur, and the real Britain of around 500 AD where his story began. A calm history for sleep about hill forts and bards, about how a little-known Welsh hero became the most famous king in the world, and about the stories that grew around him.
Visit the great timber hall on the hill of Cadbury and the rocky headland of Tintagel, listen to the bards by the fire, meet Merlin and the two dragons, see the sword pulled from the stone and Excalibur rising from the lake, sit at the Round Table in Camelot, follow Gawain through the winter snow and the knights on the quest for the Grail, and drift with the barge to the misty isle of Avalon.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
