Drift off to the story of life in ancient Greece, in Athens in 432 BC, the golden age of Pericles, in the summer the Parthenon was finished. A calm history for sleep.
Climb the Acropolis, wander through the Agora, spend an ordinary day with a family of potters, recline at a symposium, listen to Socrates and the plays of Sophocles, vote on the Pnyx, follow the Panathenaic procession, travel to the Olympic Games, and walk through Athens as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
