Drift off to life in Regency England in 1811, the year Jane Austen's first novel was published, seen through the eyes of a vicar's family in a Hampshire village not far from her cottage at Chawton. A calm history for sleep about parsonages and country lanes, muslin dresses and morning calls, letters, balls and long walks.
Visit Jane Austen at her tiny writing table with the creaking door, sew a new dress for a first ball, dance the country dances at the assembly rooms, walk through a great country house and its park, travel by stagecoach to Bath and drink the waters in the Pump Room, see the gas lamps of London, watch the Great Comet of 1811 through a telescope, welcome a brother home from the sea, and cut the Twelfth Night cake.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
