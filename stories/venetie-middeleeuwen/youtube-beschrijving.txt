Drift off to the story of life in medieval Venice around 1300, the rich city built on the water of a quiet lagoon. A calm history for sleep about glassmakers, boatmen, markets and festivals.
Meet a family of glassmakers on the island of Murano, row across the lagoon to the great market at Rialto, visit the golden church of San Marco and the shipyard of the Arsenal, hear Marco Polo tell of China, watch the doge marry the sea, and wait through a long winter for the galleys to come home from Alexandria.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
