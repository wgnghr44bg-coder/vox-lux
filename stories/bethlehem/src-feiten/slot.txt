So we leave Bethlehem as night settles over the hills of Judea.

The flocks are resting in their stone folds, and the shepherds pull their cloaks around them by the fire. In the little houses, the oil lamps flicker low, and the bread for tomorrow waits beneath a cloth. Above the quiet village, one bright star shines among the many.

Should sleep be slow to come, another story from the age of olive groves and oil lamps is there on the screen.

Sleep well, and good night.
