Drift off to the story of Bethlehem two thousand years ago, the little village in the hills of Judea where, as the Gospels tell, the first Christmas took place. A calm history for sleep.
Spend quiet days in a stone village of shepherds and farmers, bake bread in the courtyard, walk to Jerusalem for the festivals, travel the long road from Nazareth with Mary and Joseph, keep watch with the shepherds on a winter night, follow the wise men and their star, and visit Bethlehem as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
