Drift off to the calm, true history of Bethlehem at the time of the first Christmas, a small village of stone houses in the hills of Judea two thousand years ago.
Discover its houses, fields and shepherds, the old stories of Ruth and King David, the world of King Herod and Rome, the journey from Nazareth, the manger, the shepherds and the wise men, and Bethlehem's long history since.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
