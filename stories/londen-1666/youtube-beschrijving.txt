Drift off to the story of London in 1666, the old timber city of Samuel Pepys and King Charles II, in the warm, dry summer before the Great Fire. A calm history for sleep.
Wander through the crowded lanes, the markets and coffee houses, ride the river with the Thames watermen, spend ordinary days with Pepys and his wife Elizabeth, visit the old cathedral of Saint Paul's and the new theatres, follow the four days of the fire gently and from a distance, and see how Christopher Wren built a new city of brick and stone.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
