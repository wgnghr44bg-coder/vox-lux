Drift off to the calm, true story of the Great Fire of London in September 1666, and the city it swept away.
Walk through Pudding Lane, the crowded houses on London Bridge, the markets of Cheapside and the coffeehouses of the 1660s, follow Samuel Pepys and his famous diary, and see how a new London of brick and stone rose from the ashes.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
