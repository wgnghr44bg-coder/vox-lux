And so the old city of 1666 settles back into the quiet of history.

The ashes have cooled, and new brick houses rise along the narrow lanes. On the river, the watermen row slowly home under the arches of the bridge. In a small room near the Tower, a candle burns low beside an open diary, and the last ink dries on the page.

Let the whole busy city loosen its grip now. The streets, the bells and the voices can all drift gently away into the dark.

Whenever sleep allows, another calm story from a distant century is resting on the screen. Pressing subscribe lets the following evening find its way to you.

Sleep well, and good night.
