Drift off to the story of the great San Francisco earthquake and fire of April 1906, in the city by the Golden Gate.
Wander through the gold rush harbour, ride the cable cars and hear Caruso sing on the last evening, then follow the people of San Francisco as they help each other through three days of fire and slowly build their city again.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
and softly changing images.
