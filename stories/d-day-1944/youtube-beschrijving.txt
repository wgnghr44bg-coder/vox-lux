Drift off to the story of the days and the night before D-Day, in June 1944, told gently from the waiting: the soldiers in their camps and ships, the weathermen, the general, and the families on both sides of the sea. A calm history for sleep.
Walk the lanes of southern England full of soldiers, meet the American GIs and a village boy, step into the sealed camps, hear about Gustav the pigeon and the secret crossword, see the harbours fill with the greatest fleet in history, wait through the storm with weatherman James Stagg and Maureen at the lighthouse in Ireland, sit with Eisenhower at Southwick House, listen to the secret poem on the radio in France, cross the sea by night, stand in the garden under a sky full of aircraft, and visit Pegasus Bridge, Bayeux and Normandy today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
