Drift off to the story of the building of Stonehenge, around 2500 BC, when the farmers and herders of Neolithic Britain raised great stones on Salisbury Plain and gathered for midwinter feasts. A calm history for sleep, just before the summer solstice.
Walk the chalk plain, see the first ring of earth, follow the bluestones on their long journey from the hills of Wales, meet a family travelling to the midwinter feast at Durrington Walls, help pull a great sarsen from West Woods, watch the stones being raised and the lintels locked together like wood, stand inside the ring at the solstices, walk the Avenue to the river, visit Woodhenge and the stone villages of Orkney, meet the Amesbury Archer, and see how Stonehenge is understood today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
