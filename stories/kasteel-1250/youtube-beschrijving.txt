Drift off to the story of life inside a medieval castle in 1250, at Kenilworth, the red stone fortress on its lake, home of Simon de Montfort and Countess Eleanor. A calm history for sleep.
Walk through the gatehouse and the great hall, follow an ordinary day from the bakehouse at dawn to the guards on the walls at night, sit down to dinner with the household, train with the squires, ride out with the hawks and hounds, and see the castle as it stands today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
