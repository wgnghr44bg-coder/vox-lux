Drift off to life in America during the Great Depression in 1933, told gently through the eyes of a farm family in Iowa. A calm history for sleep about making do, helping the neighbours, gathering around the radio and finding hope in the hardest of times.
Do the morning chores by lantern light, drive the old Model T into town on Saturday, listen to Roosevelt's first fireside chat, sew a dress from printed flour sacks, give a meal to a traveller at the back door, stand silently together at a penny auction, ride the Sky Ride at the Chicago world's fair, win a red ribbon at the county fair, husk corn by hand in the autumn fields, and share Thanksgiving with everyone who needs a seat at the table.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
