We have followed the hard years of the Depression, from closed banks and penny auctions to radio evenings, county fairs and the first electric lights. Through it all, families held on, and neighbours helped each other.

Now the lamps in the farmhouse windows go dark. The cornfields lie silent under the stars, and the windmill stands still. None of it needs to be remembered.

For anyone still awake, another slow journey into the past is waiting there on the screen. A subscription will bring the next one close.

Let your breathing settle, and let the quiet prairie night carry you away.

Sleep well, and good night.
