Drift off to the great voyage of the Polynesian navigators, sailing a double canoe from Raiatea across thousands of kilometres of open ocean to Hawaii, around the year 1000 AD, told gently through the eyes of an old navigator and his granddaughter. A calm history for sleep about stars, swells, seabirds and the quiet courage of the greatest sailors of the ancient world.
Watch the canoe being carved and lashed with coconut cord, weave the pandanus sails, learn the star compass on the beach at night, pack the taro and breadfruit for a new land, sail through the calms at the equator, meet dolphins and a curious whale, see the North Star rise from the sea, follow the terns to land, and step ashore beneath a snow-capped mountain.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
