Drift off to the calm, true history of the alchemists and their search for the philosopher's stone, the legendary substance said to turn lead into gold, at its height around the year 1600.
Travel from ancient Alexandria and Baghdad to candlelit European workshops, the court of Emperor Rudolf II in Prague, John Dee and Edward Kelley, gilded gallows, women alchemists, Robert Boyle and Isaac Newton, and the birth of modern chemistry.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
