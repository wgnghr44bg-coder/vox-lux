Drift off to Florence and the Tuscan hills in 1347 and 1348, and discover how ordinary people lived through the Black Death and found their way back to everyday life. A calm history for sleep.
Walk the streets of medieval Florence with the wool workers and the merchants, meet the writers Boccaccio, Villani and Petrarch, follow the great sickness gently and from a distance, listen to the young storytellers of the Decameron in their villa on the hill, and see the kindness, weddings and new beginnings that followed, all the way to Florence today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
