Drift off to the story of Europe in the time of the Black Death, 1348, told through the medieval city of Florence and the writer Giovanni Boccaccio. A calm history for sleep.
Wander through the stone streets and markets of Florence, spend quiet days with wool workers, merchants and farmers in the Tuscan hills, meet Boccaccio, the chronicler Villani and the poet Petrarch, follow the great sickness gently and from a distance, listen to stories from the Decameron, and see how Europe slowly began again.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
