Drift off to the story of Tudor England under Henry VIII in 1520, when the young king was golden-haired and full of music, and the court crossed to France for the Field of the Cloth of Gold. A calm history for sleep.
Walk through Cardinal Wolsey's new palace at Hampton Court, spend a day with an apprentice in Cheapside, visit a village with its maypole and archery butts, sit at a Tudor feast, meet Thomas More and Catherine of Aragon, ride to the golden tents and wine fountains in France, and visit Tudor England as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
