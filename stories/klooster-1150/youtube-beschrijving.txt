Drift off to the story of a medieval monastery in 1150, Rievaulx Abbey in a quiet Yorkshire valley, where the white monks lived a life of prayer, work and silence under their kind abbot Aelred. A calm history for sleep.
Rise with the monks for the night office by candlelight, follow their long quiet day of chant and work, visit the cloister, the refectory, the scriptorium and the infirmary, spend a day with the lay brothers among the sheep on the moors, and walk through the ruins of the abbey as they stand today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
