Drift off to the story of the Titanic, the great liner that sailed from Southampton in April 1912 and was lost in the North Atlantic on her first voyage.
Walk through the Belfast shipyard where she was built, wander her decks with passengers from every class, and follow the quiet night of the fifteenth of April, the rescue by the Carpathia, and the finding of the wreck, gently and without frightening details.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
and softly changing images.
