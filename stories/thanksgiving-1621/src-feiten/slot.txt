We have travelled along the coast of the People of the First Light, through the small village at Plymouth, and into the three days of the harvest of 1621. We have seen how a few lines in an old letter grew into a holiday.

Now the fires along the shore burn low. The bay grows dark, and the geese settle on the quiet marsh. None of it has to stay with you.

If sleep has not come yet, another calm journey into history is waiting for you on the screen. Subscribing keeps the next one close by.

Breathe in slowly, and let the autumn night carry you away.

Sleep well, and good night.
