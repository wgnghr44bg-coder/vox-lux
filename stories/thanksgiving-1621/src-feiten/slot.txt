And so we leave Plymouth, at the end of that first autumn.

The fires in the little houses have burned down to soft red embers. In the woods, the long houses are quiet, and only a thin line of smoke rises from each roof. The harbour lies dark and still. Somewhere out over the bay, a flock of geese calls once, very softly, and then the night is silent again.
