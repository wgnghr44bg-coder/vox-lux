Drift off to the real story of the first Thanksgiving, the harvest celebration of autumn 1621 in Plymouth, told as a calm history for sleep.
Walk the shores of the Wampanoag, the People of the First Light, meet Massasoit and Tisquantum, spend busy autumn days in the tiny English village, go fowling with the four hunters, share three days of venison, corn and games, and follow how a small harvest feast became a national holiday.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
