Drift off to the story of life in Ottoman Istanbul in 1550, in the golden age of Sultan Suleiman the Magnificent, seen through the eyes of a stonemason's family. A calm history for sleep about a great city on two continents, full of domes, gardens and many peoples.
Watch the great Süleymaniye mosque rise under the architect Sinan, wander through the Grand Bazaar, cross the Golden Horn by caique to Galata, relax in the steam of a hammam, stand beneath the dome of Hagia Sophia, see the sultan ride by on a white horse, picnic among the tulips, sail up the Bosphorus with the dolphins, and share the warm evenings of Ramadan.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
