So we leave Istanbul tonight, in the year 1550, as the evening falls. The sun is setting behind the old city. The domes and the minarets stand dark against the orange sky. The water of the Golden Horn is turning gold, and then pink. The last caiques are crossing to Galata, their oars dipping softly in the water.

--

From the minarets, the evening call to prayer drifts out over the rooftops. One voice, and then another, and then hundreds, rising and falling, echoing across the water. In the streets, the shopkeepers are closing their shutters. The porters are going home. The cats are curling up on the warm stones, for the night.

{img:wooden-house-lattice-window-lamp-glow-evening-narrow-street-cat}
In the mahalle near the Golden Horn, the lamps are being lit, one by one, behind the wooden lattices. In the little courtyard with the fig tree, Ayşe is folding her embroidery and putting it away. Nine is sitting by the window, her eyes half closed, listening to the sounds of the street. And Fatma is putting her red slippers carefully back in their box, for the next special day.

--

Mehmet and his father are washing the stone dust from their hands at the fountain. The water is cool and clear. Above them, on the third hill, the half-built walls of the great mosque stand silent in the dusk. The cranes are still. The scaffolding is empty. The stones are waiting for tomorrow, when the hammers will ring again.

{img:night-istanbul-1550-moon-over-domes-bosporus-silver-water-quiet}
Out on the Bosphorus, the water flows quietly between Europe and Asia, as it has for thousands of years. The fishing boats are coming home. The dolphins are swimming somewhere in the dark water. On the hills of both shores, little lights are burning in the windows of the villages. And over everything, the moon is rising, white and calm, over the domes of the sleeping city.

--

The city grows quiet. The voices fade. The last lamps go out, one by one. Somewhere, a boat bell rings softly. Somewhere, a mother sings a lullaby, in Turkish, or Greek, or Armenian, or Spanish. And the great city of Istanbul, on its seven hills between two seas, falls asleep under the stars.
