Most of the stone for the pyramid comes from right next door.

Just south of the pyramid, the builders cut a great quarry into the hill. It is a huge hole in the rock, many metres deep. You can still see it today. From this quarry come most of the blocks that make up the inside of the pyramid. More than two million of them.

--

How do they cut the stone? They have no iron. Their tools are made of copper, a soft reddish metal, and of stone. They have copper chisels and saws. They have wooden mallets and stone hammers. And they have wooden wedges.

First they mark out a block on the rock with red paint. Then they cut deep narrow channels around it with copper chisels. It is slow work. The copper wears down quickly. The tool makers must sharpen the chisels again and again. Then the workers knock wooden wedges under the block. Sometimes they pour water on the wedges. The wood swells. And with a crack, the block comes free from the rock.

{img:workers-levering-block-free-wooden-wedges-quarry-floor-shade}
An average block weighs about two and a half tons. That is about as heavy as two cars. Some are much bigger. The workers lever them out with long wooden poles. They tie ropes around them. And they slide them onto wooden sledges, ready to be pulled to the pyramid.

--

It is hot work and dusty work. The quarry is a deep hole that traps the sun. The air is full of white dust. It gets in the workers' eyes and noses and mouths. They tie cloths over their faces. Water carriers walk among them with big clay jars. And every few hours, the overseers call a rest in the shade.

{img:water-carrier-clay-jars-shoulder-yoke-quarry-workers-drinking}
The quarry workers are skilled men. They know how to read the rock. They can tell where it will split cleanly and where it will crack badly. A bad crack can ruin a block. And sometimes the rock is full of hidden cracks and shells and soft patches. Then the workers must find a better place to cut. Every day, the block counter writes down how many blocks are ready. And every day, Hemiunu wants more.

--

The quarry grows bigger every year. It eats into the hill, deeper and wider. The stone that comes out of it goes straight into the pyramid next door. So in a way, the hill is slowly moving. Block by block, it is being lifted up and stacked into a new shape. When the work is done, there will be a great hole on one side, and a great mountain on the other. Some people today say the pyramid is the hill, turned inside out and piled up toward the sky.
