To build the pyramid, Khufu needs a master builder. He chooses a member of his own family. His name is Hemiunu.

Hemiunu is the king's nephew. He has the title of overseer of all the king's works. That means he is in charge of everything. The plans and the workers. The stone and the boats. The bread and the beer. It is a huge task. One mistake, and the whole project could fail.

--

We know what Hemiunu looked like. His statue was found in his own tomb near the pyramid. He is shown sitting down. He is quite a big man, with a round belly and soft arms. In ancient Egypt, that showed you were rich and well fed. His face is calm and serious. He looks like a man who thinks carefully before he speaks.

Hemiunu and his team plan everything with great care. The pyramid will stand on a square base. Each side will be about two hundred and thirty metres long. That is longer than two football fields. And the pyramid will be about a hundred and forty-six metres high. That is as high as a building of forty floors.

{img:egyptian-surveyors-stars-measuring-north-ropes-plumb-lines-night}
The four sides must face exactly north, south, east and west. How did they find true north with no compass? They probably used the stars. At night they watched certain stars circling the sky around the north. They marked where those stars rose and set. And halfway between, they found north. They got it almost perfectly right. The error is less than a tenth of a degree.

--

The base must also be perfectly flat. Across more than five hectares of rock. If one corner were even a little higher than the others, the pyramid would lean. The builders may have cut little channels in the rock and filled them with water. Water lies perfectly flat. They marked the water line. Then they cut the rock down to that line. In the end, the base is level to within about two centimetres, from one corner to the other.

{img:workers-cutting-rock-base-level-channels-water-giza-plateau-sun}
Inside the pyramid, Hemiunu plans corridors and rooms. A chamber for the king's coffin. A great sloping hall. Narrow shafts that point toward the stars. Every block must fit. Every angle must be right. And everything must be ready in time.
