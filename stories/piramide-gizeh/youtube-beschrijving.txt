Drift off to the story of the building of the Great Pyramid of Giza, around 2560 BC, when thousands of Egyptian workers raised the tallest building on earth for King Khufu. A calm history for sleep.
Meet King Khufu and his master builder Hemiunu, visit the quarries and the copper workshops, spend time in the workers' town with its bakeries and doctors, sail with Merer, whose diary is the oldest papyrus ever found, carry white stone from Tura and granite from Aswan on the Nile flood, pull a sledge up the ramps, step inside the Grand Gallery and the King's Chamber, see Khufu's cedar boat, and visit Giza as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
