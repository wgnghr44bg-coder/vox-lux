That was the winter of a medieval village: the stored grain and salted pork, the smoky hearth and the rushlight, the shepherd in the snow, the carols at Christmas, and the first lambs of spring. It was also the story of a village that was later emptied, and found again by patient diggers.

Now the valley grows dark. The frost settles on the old church, and the sheep rest on the quiet slopes. All of it can simply drift away.
