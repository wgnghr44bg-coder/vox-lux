Drift off to the story of how medieval peasants survived the long winter, around the year 1300, in the quiet village of Wharram Percy in the Yorkshire Wolds. A calm history for sleep.
Spend a whole winter with one peasant family in their thatched longhouse: preparing for the cold, going to market, cooking pottage by the hearth, caring for the oxen, celebrating the twelve days of Christmas, telling stories by the fire, playing in the snow and waiting for spring, and visit the lost village as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
