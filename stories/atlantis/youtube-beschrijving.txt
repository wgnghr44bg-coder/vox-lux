Drift off to the legend of Atlantis, the lost island first described by the Greek philosopher Plato around 360 BC, and to the real places and discoveries that may lie behind it. A calm history for sleep.
Sit in Plato's garden school in Athens, follow the story from the priests of Saïs in Egypt to the wise Solon, walk through the city of rings of land and water, its silver temple of Poseidon and its great plain, meet the ten kings and their bulls, hear how the island vanished in one day and one night, and then search for Atlantis yourself: the volcano of Thera, the painted town of Akrotiri, the Minoans of Crete, the sunken town of Helike, and Doggerland under the North Sea.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
