If Atlantis was not completely made up, what could it be? People have looked for answers in many places. One of the most exciting is an island in the Aegean Sea. Today it is called Santorini. In ancient times it was called Thera.

--

Santorini is a beautiful island today. White houses with blue doors cling to the top of high cliffs. Below them, there is a great bay of deep blue water. In the middle of the bay there are small dark islands. Visitors come from all over the world to watch the sunset there.

But that bay was not always there. Long ago, Thera was a round island. With a volcano in the middle. And around it, people lived and farmed and fished. It was a green and pleasant place. With a busy harbour town.

{img:volcano-smoke-rising-thera-island-people-boats-leaving-calm-sea}
Then, about three thousand six hundred years ago, the volcano woke up. First there were small earthquakes. Then smoke. The people saw the signs. And it seems they left in time. Because archaeologists have found their town, but almost no people. They took their valuable things. And they sailed away.

--

Then the volcano erupted. It was one of the biggest eruptions in the history of humans. Ash and stone shot high into the sky. The ash spread across the whole sea. It fell on islands far away. It is found in the ground on Crete and in Turkey and even in Egypt. The sky went dark for days.

{img:great-ash-cloud-over-aegean-sea-distant-islands-dim-sun-gentle}
And the middle of the island collapsed. The volcano emptied itself and then fell in. The sea rushed into the great hole. Where there had been a round island with a mountain, there was now a ring of cliffs around a deep bay. And waves raced out across the sea toward other islands.

--

Doesn't that sound like Atlantis? A round island. Rings of land and water. Earthquakes. A great disaster. Part of the island sinking into the sea. Many people have thought so. And the bay of Santorini today looks a little like a ring of land around a ring of water.

{img:santorini-caldera-today-white-houses-cliffs-blue-bay-sunset-later}
But there are problems. Plato says Atlantis was beyond the Pillars of Heracles, in the outer ocean. Thera is inside the inner sea. Plato says Atlantis was huge. Thera is small. Plato says it happened nine thousand years before Solon. Thera erupted about a thousand years before Solon. Some people think the numbers got mixed up as the story was told and retold. Perhaps nine hundred became nine thousand. Who knows?
