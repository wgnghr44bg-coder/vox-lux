After Plato, people kept on wondering about Atlantis. But for many centuries, most of them agreed with Aristotle. They said it was a story, a nice one with a good lesson, and nothing more.

Then, about a hundred and fifty years ago, everything changed.

--

In 1882, an American politician called Ignatius Donnelly wrote a book. It was called Atlantis, the Antediluvian World, which means the world before the flood. In his book, Donnelly said that Atlantis had been completely real. He said it had been a great continent in the middle of the Atlantic Ocean. He said it was the mother of all the old civilisations of the world. From Atlantis, he wrote, people had sailed to Egypt and to America and everywhere else. That was why the pyramids of Egypt and the pyramids of Mexico look a little alike, he said. They had both come from Atlantis.

His book was a huge success, and millions of people read it. People read it by the fire in the evenings and talked about it at dinner. And suddenly, everyone was talking about Atlantis again.

{img:explorers-ship-searching-ocean-atlantic-sonar-map-early-modern}
After Donnelly's book, people began to find Atlantis everywhere they looked. They found it in the Atlantic and in the Mediterranean. They found it in the Caribbean and in the cold Arctic. They found it in Africa and in South America, and even under the ice of Antarctica. They found it in Spain and in Ireland and in Sweden. Every few years, someone says they have found it at last. And every time, it turns out not to be Atlantis after all.

--

Scientists who study the sea floor say there was never a big continent in the middle of the Atlantic. The floor of the ocean there is slowly spreading apart. New sea floor is made in the middle, very slowly, year after year. There is simply no room for a sunken continent there. And there is no sign of one.

But people keep looking, because the idea is so wonderful. A lost world, a great civilisation forgotten under the sea, and secrets waiting to be found. It is one of the best stories ever told.

{img:diver-underwater-ancient-stones-sea-floor-light-rays-searching}
Atlantis has appeared in countless books and films and games and cartoons. There are underwater cities with glass domes, and people who can breathe under water. There are glowing crystals and lost machines, mermaids and sea kings. Most of it has nothing to do with Plato at all. But all of it began with his unfinished story, in a garden of olive trees outside Athens.

--

Some of the searchers have been very serious about it. They have spent years of their lives and a great deal of money. They have hired ships and divers and aeroplanes. In the Bahamas, divers once found a long line of flat stones under the clear water. Some people called it the road to Atlantis. But geologists looked at it carefully and said it was a natural rock formation. The searchers were disappointed for a while. And then they packed their bags and went searching somewhere else.

Many of these searchers were quite happy people, in their own way. They loved the sea and the sunshine and the long days on the boat. They loved reading old maps by lamplight in the evenings. Even if they never found Atlantis, they found adventures and friends along the way.
