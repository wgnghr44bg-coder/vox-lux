So we leave the Tower as the evening settles over the Thames.

The gates are locked, and the keys are safely put away. In the little houses along the walls, families bank their fires for the night. The lions doze in their stone dens, the ravens tuck their heads beneath their wings, and the river slides quietly past the old wharf.

For those who are still awake, another gentle story from the Tudor age and beyond rests on the screen nearby.

Sleep well, and good night.
