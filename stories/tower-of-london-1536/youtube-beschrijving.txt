Drift off to the calm, true history of life in the Tower of London in 1536, the year of Henry VIII, Anne Boleyn and Thomas Cromwell.
Walk through the White Tower and the royal palace, meet the Constable and the Yeoman Warders, the families who lived inside the walls, the coin makers of the Royal Mint and the lions of the royal menagerie, and follow the prisoners, the spring of 1536 and the ravens and ceremonies that still continue today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
