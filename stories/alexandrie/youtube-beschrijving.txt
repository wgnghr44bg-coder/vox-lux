Drift off to the story of life in ancient Alexandria around 240 BC, the white city by the sea with the greatest library of the ancient world and the famous lighthouse of Pharos. A calm history for sleep about books, scholars and the sea.
Meet a Greek copyist and his Egyptian wife who sells papyrus, their daughter who loves to read and their son who loves the ships. Walk through the library and the Mouseion, see how papyrus is made from the reeds of the Nile, climb the great lighthouse, watch Eratosthenes measure the whole Earth with a stick and a shadow, and see the light of the Pharos guide a ship home through a storm.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
