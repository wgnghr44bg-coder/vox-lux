Drift off to life in Petra in the year 100 AD, the rose-red city of the Nabataeans, carved into the cliffs of the desert, told gently through the eyes of a frankincense merchant's family. A calm history for sleep about caravans, cool courtyards, clever water channels and a hidden city glowing in the evening light.
Walk through the shadowy Siq to the Treasury, learn how the desert city found its water, test frankincense in a merchant's storeroom, welcome a thousand camels from the south, bargain in the colonnaded market, laugh at a play in the rock-cut theatre, paint an eggshell-thin bowl, climb the eight hundred steps to the Monastery, sleep under the desert stars, and follow the explorer who found Petra again in 1812.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
