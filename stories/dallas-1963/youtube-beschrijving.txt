Drift off to the story of Dallas, Texas, in the autumn of 1963, the bright and growing city where President John F. Kennedy came to visit in November, told as a calm history for sleep.
Spend quiet days with an ordinary Dallas family, with the dressmaker Abraham Zapruder and the young Newman family, follow the President and Jacqueline Kennedy across Texas, and then watch the long weekend gently and from a distance, before you walk through Dealey Plaza as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
