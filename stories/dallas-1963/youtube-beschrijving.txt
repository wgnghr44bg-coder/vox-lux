Drift off to the story of Dallas, Texas, in the autumn of 1963, the bright, growing city where President John F. Kennedy came to visit on the twenty-second of November. A calm history for sleep.
Spend quiet days with an ordinary Dallas family, with the dressmaker Abraham Zapruder and the young Newman family, ride with the President and Jacqueline Kennedy from Fort Worth to Love Field, follow that day and the long weekend gently and from a distance, and walk through Dealey Plaza as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
