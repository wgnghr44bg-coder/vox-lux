BODY: Abraham Zapruder was born in 1905 in a small town in the Russian Empire, now part of Ukraine. His family was Jewish. His story was typical of millions of immigrants in the early twentieth century. Many arrived with little money and built steady lives through hard work in industries such as clothing manufacturing. Most remained unknown outside their own families. Zapruder's name became famous only because of a few seconds of home movie film he shot in November 1963.

Outside, the weather was changing. The clouds were beginning to break up and patches of blue sky were appearing. By late morning, the sun was shining brightly. The air was warm and fresh, and the pavements steamed a little as the rain dried. At about half past eleven, Air Force One took off from Fort Worth for the shortest flight of the trip. It would take only about thirteen minutes. From the window, the President and the First Lady could see the flat brown plains of Texas, crossed with roads, and dotted with farms and small towns. And then, ahead, the shining towers of Dallas.
