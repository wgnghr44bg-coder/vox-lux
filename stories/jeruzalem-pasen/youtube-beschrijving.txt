Drift off to the story of Jerusalem at the first Easter, in the spring of 33 AD, when the holy city filled with pilgrims for Passover and a teacher from Galilee rode in on a donkey. A calm history for sleep.
Walk through the golden city and its great Temple, join the pilgrims on the roads and the families at the Passover meal, follow the week from Palm Sunday to the garden of Gethsemane, the quiet Sabbath and the first Easter morning, told gently and with reverence, and visit Jerusalem as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
