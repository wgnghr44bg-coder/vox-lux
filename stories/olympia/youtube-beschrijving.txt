Drift off to the story of the first Olympic Games in ancient Greece, from the single footrace of 776 BC to the great festival of Zeus at Olympia. A calm history for sleep, seen through the eyes of a shepherd boy from the mountains of Arcadia who travels to Olympia to run.
Hear the heralds announce the sacred truce, train for a month in Elis, walk with the procession to the valley of Olympia, stand before the great golden statue of Zeus, sleep in the tent camp by the river, watch the chariots race in the hippodrome, join the feast under the full moon, meet the girls who ran for the goddess Hera, and see the winners crowned with wreaths of olive leaves.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
