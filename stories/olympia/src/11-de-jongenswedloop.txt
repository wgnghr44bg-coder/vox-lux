On the morning of the second day, Lykos wakes up before sunrise. For a moment, he does not remember where he is. Then he hears the sounds of the camp, and he remembers. Today is the day of the boys' race. His stomach is full of butterflies. He can hardly eat his breakfast. Philon makes him eat a little bread with honey anyway.

--

The boys' contests are held in the stadium. There is the boys' race, boys' wrestling and boys' boxing. Early in the morning, the crowd begins to fill the grassy banks. The people bring cushions to sit on, and hats against the sun, and jars of water. Soon the banks are completely full. Thousands and thousands of people, talking, laughing, eating and waiting.

On one side of the stadium, there is a little stone platform. This is where the judges sit, in their purple robes. On the other side, there is an altar. And beside the altar sits a woman, dressed in white. She is the priestess of the goddess Demeter. She is the only married woman who is allowed to watch the Games. All the others must stay away.

Lykos stands at the end of the track with the other boys. There are about twenty of them. They are oiled and ready. The judges have divided them into groups, by drawing lots from a silver jar. Each group will run a race. And the winners of each group will run again, in a final race. Lykos is in the third group. Dion is in the first.

Lykos watches Dion run. The trumpet sounds. The boys run. Dion runs well, but he is beaten by a tall boy from Sparta. He comes back with his head down. Lykos puts his hand on his shoulder. Dion smiles bravely and says, now you must win for both of us.

--

{img:boys-crouched-stone-starting-line-toes-in-grooves-tense-moment}
Then it is Lykos's turn. He walks to the starting line, with five other boys. He puts his toes into the grooves in the stone, as Philon taught him. He leans forward, with his arms stretched out in front of him. He looks down the long track. It seems very long, and very bright in the morning sun. The crowd goes quiet.

Lykos breathes in slowly. He thinks of the mountains, and the goats, and the green field by the stream. He thinks of his mother, and Theano. He can feel his heart beating in his chest. The herald calls out the names of the runners. Lykos, son of Kleon, from Arcadia. Lykos does not hear it. He is listening only for the trumpet.

{img:boy-runners-sprinting-stadium-dust-flying-crowd-standing-cheering}
The trumpet sounds. And Lykos runs. He runs the way he has always run, in the mountains, after the goats. Light and fast, his feet barely touching the ground. He does not look at the other boys. He just runs. The crowd roars. The end of the track comes closer and closer. And Lykos crosses the line first, two steps ahead of everyone else.

He can hardly believe it. He has won his group. But it is not over yet. There is still the final. Philon gives him water and tells him to rest, and breathe slowly. Lykos sits down in the shade, his heart pounding. He looks at the other winners. One of them is the tall Spartan boy, who beat Dion.

--

The final race comes in the afternoon. Six boys stand at the starting line. The crowd is louder now. The judges lean forward on their platform. The trumpet sounds. And the boys are off. This time, the Spartan boy is fast, very fast. Halfway down the track, he is ahead. Lykos runs harder than ever before. His legs burn. His chest burns. Slowly, slowly, he catches up. And at the very end, he leans forward, as Philon taught him, and crosses the line, half a step in front.

{img:boy-winner-palm-branch-red-ribbons-judges-crowd-joy-afternoon}
For a moment, there is silence. Then the herald stands up, and calls out in his great voice. The winner of the boys' race is Lykos, son of Kleon, from Arcadia! The crowd cheers. A judge gives Lykos a palm branch, the sign of a winner. People throw flowers and ribbons. His father runs down onto the track and lifts him up in his arms. Old Philon is crying openly. And Dion is jumping up and down on the bank, shouting his name. The olive wreath will be given on the last day. But today, Lykos is a champion of Olympia.
