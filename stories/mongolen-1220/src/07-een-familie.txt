Let us meet a family on the steppe in 1220.

They live in the green valley by the river. There are three gers close together. One for the grandparents. One for the family. One for storage and guests. Around them graze their animals. About two hundred sheep and goats. Twenty cows. Thirty horses. And four camels for moving.

--

The father is called Batu. He is not at home. He rode west with the army more than a year ago. He is one of the khan's riders. He sends news when he can, through the messengers. The last news came three months ago. He was well. He was far away, in a land of great rivers and cities. Nobody at home can really imagine it.

The mother is called Sarnai. That means rose. She runs everything while Batu is away. The herds. The family. The camp. Mongol women have always had a lot of responsibility. When the men are away, the women are in charge. And they are very good at it.

{img:sarnai-mother-checking-herd-hillside-horse-whip-wind-hair}
There are four children. The eldest is a girl of fourteen, called Khulan. She is a wonderful rider. Next is a boy of eleven, called Temur. He wants to be a warrior like his father. Then a girl of seven, called Altan, which means gold. And a little boy of three, called Bayar. That means joy. He is always laughing.

--

Batu's mother lives with them too. She is old now, about seventy. Her face is brown and lined like an old map. She cannot ride as she used to. But she knows everything about animals and weather and herbs. She knows all the old stories. And she watches over the little ones.

{img:grandmother-teaching-girl-herbs-steppe-flowers-basket-summer}
There is also a dog. A big shaggy black dog with a brown spot over each eye. People say the spots are like a second pair of eyes. So the dog can see danger even when it is asleep. It guards the camp at night. And it barks at every wolf and every stranger.

--

Sarnai worries about her husband. She does not say it. But every time a rider appears on the horizon, she stops what she is doing. She shades her eyes and looks. Is it a messenger? Is there news? Usually it is just a neighbour. And she goes back to work. But she keeps looking.
