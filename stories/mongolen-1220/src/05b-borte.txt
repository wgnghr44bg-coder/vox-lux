When Temüjin was about sixteen, he rode back to Börte's family. He had not forgotten the girl he was promised to. And she had not forgotten him.

They married. Her family gave a wonderful gift. A coat of black sable fur. It was very precious. Temüjin had very little. But with that coat, he went to visit a powerful chief who had been his father's friend. He gave him the coat as a gift. And the chief agreed to help him. It was the beginning of Temüjin's rise.

--

But soon after the wedding, danger came. Riders from another tribe attacked the camp at dawn. Everyone fled. Temüjin escaped into the mountains. But Börte was caught. She was carried away. Temüjin was heartbroken. He prayed on the sacred mountain. And then he gathered his friends and allies. Months later, they rode out to bring her back.

The story says it was night when they reached the enemy camp. People fled in the dark. Temüjin rode through the chaos, calling her name. Börte! Börte! And in the moonlight, Börte heard his voice. She ran toward him. She grabbed the reins of his horse. And he knew her. They held each other. The old book says, they were together again, like before.

{img:night-reunion-moonlight-rider-woman-holding-reins-steppe-camp}
Börte became Temüjin's first wife and his most trusted adviser. She was wise and calm and clever. When he was unsure, he listened to her. Once, she warned him that a close friend could no longer be trusted. He listened. She was right.

--

They had four sons and several daughters. Their sons became great rulers after their father. Their daughters married the chiefs of other peoples. And they were given great power. Some ruled whole lands while their husbands were at war. Genghis Khan trusted his daughters very much.

{img:borte-managing-camp-giving-orders-women-officials-gers-busy}
In 1220, Börte is about sixty years old. Her husband is far away in the west, with the armies. He has been gone for over a year. She stays in Mongolia, at the great camp. She runs things at home. She looks after the family and the herds and the camps. She makes decisions. She welcomes visitors. Messengers come and go. And every day she waits for news from the west.
