Drift off to the story of life in the Mongol Empire in 1220, in the time of Genghis Khan, told from the steppe at home, where families lived with their horses and herds while the riders were far away in the west. A calm history for sleep.
Step inside a felt ger, help make felt and airag, ride the small tough Mongol horses, hear how the hungry boy Temüjin became Genghis Khan with Börte at his side, spend a day with a herding family through the seasons, ride with the messengers of the Yam, meet the traders of the Silk Road, visit Karakorum and the sacred mountain, watch the summer games and the eagle hunters, follow the old monk Changchun on his long journey to the khan, live through a hard winter, and visit Mongolia today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
