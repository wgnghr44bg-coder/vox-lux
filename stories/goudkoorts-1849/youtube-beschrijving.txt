Drift off to the story of life in the California Gold Rush of 1849, when people from all over the world came to look for gold in the rivers of the Sierra Nevada foothills. A calm history for sleep about hope, hard work and finding a new home.
Sail with a carpenter and his fourteen-year-old son from Massachusetts around Cape Horn, arrive in the tent city of San Francisco, travel up the rivers to a mining camp, learn to wash gold with a pan and a rocker, meet miners from Mexico, Chile and China, wait for letters from home through a long rainy winter, and see the family together again at last.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
