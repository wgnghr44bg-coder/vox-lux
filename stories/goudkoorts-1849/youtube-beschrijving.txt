Drift off to the calm, true story of the California Gold Rush, from the first flakes at Sutter's Mill in January 1848 to the great rush of the forty-niners.
Follow the wagons across the plains and the ships around Cape Horn, walk the muddy streets of instant San Francisco, and spend quiet evenings in the cabins and mining camps of the Sierra foothills.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
