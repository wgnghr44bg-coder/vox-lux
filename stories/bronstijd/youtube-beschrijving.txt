Drift off to life in the Bronze Age around 1500 BC, in a village of longhouses on the heath in Denmark, in the time of the famous Sun Chariot and the Nebra Sky Disc. A calm history for sleep about a farming family, the golden amber of the north, the shining new metal from the south, and a people who honoured the sun and the stars.
Search for amber on the beach after a storm, bargain with the trader from the far south, watch the old smith cast a bronze pin from melted metal, weave the woollen clothes of the Egtved girl, watch the seven sisters to know when to harvest, sit on the burial mound of the ancestors at sunset, celebrate a midsummer wedding, bring in the barley with bronze sickles, and welcome back the sun at midwinter.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
