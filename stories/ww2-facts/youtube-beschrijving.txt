Drift off to ninety quiet facts about everyday life during the Second World War, from the first wartime Sunday by the wireless in 1939 to the street parties, the last ration book and the calm memorials of today. A calm history for sleep.
Hear about blackout curtains and garden shelters, letters from home and cups of tea, Wojtek the soldier bear, the code breakers of Bletchley Park, victory gardens in America, the Mona Lisa's long journey, the bells ringing for peace and the quiet years that followed, gently and without battles or frightening details.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
