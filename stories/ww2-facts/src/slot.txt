And so our quiet journey through the war years comes to an end.

The wireless sets have fallen silent. The blackout curtains are folded away, and in a thousand small kitchens, a kettle is warming slowly on the stove. Outside, the street lamps glow again. Above them, the stars are still there, fainter now, but still shining. The long years settle gently into memory.

If you are still awake, another quiet story is waiting for you on the screen. And if this one helped you rest, you are always welcome to subscribe.

Let your thoughts grow slow and heavy. There is nothing left to follow.

Sleep well, and good night.
