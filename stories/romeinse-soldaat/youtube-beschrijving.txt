Drift off to the story of the life of a Roman soldier around 120 AD, from a sunny town in southern Gaul to the cold, green hills of Roman Britain. A calm history for sleep about training, friendship, everyday life in a Roman fort, and the building of Hadrian's Wall.
Swear the soldier's oath, learn to march and swim, put on the armour and the hobnailed boots, share a barrack room with seven friends and a stubborn mule, cross the sea to Britain, relax in the great bathhouse, read the real letters of Vindolanda, stand before the emperor Hadrian, help build the wall across Britain, and grow old on the frontier.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
