So we leave London on Christmas night, as the snow begins to fall.

The shops are shuttered, and the markets are still. In the little houses of Camden Town, the fires burn low, and the last of the pudding waits on the shelf for tomorrow. Far across the rooftops, the bells of the old churches ring once more, and then the city settles into sleep.

If the night still feels long, another warm story from days gone by is waiting on the screen.

Sleep well, and good night.
