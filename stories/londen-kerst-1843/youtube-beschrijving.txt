Drift off to the calm, true history of Christmas in Victorian London in 1843, the year Charles Dickens published A Christmas Carol.
Walk through the foggy, gaslit streets of a city of two million, its markets, street sellers, workhouses and cosy parlours, follow Dickens as he wrote his famous story, and share the customs of a Victorian Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and Boxing Day.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
