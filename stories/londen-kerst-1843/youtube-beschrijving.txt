Drift off to the story of Victorian London at Christmas in 1843, the winter when Charles Dickens wrote A Christmas Carol. A calm history for sleep.
Walk through the foggy, gaslit streets and the great markets, spend evenings with a clerk's family in Camden Town and with the young Dickens and his children, hear the story of Scrooge, Fezziwig and Tiny Tim told gently once more, and see how Londoners kept Christmas, from the first Christmas card to the Boxing Day pantomime.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
