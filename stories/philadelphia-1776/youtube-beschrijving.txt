Drift off to the story of Philadelphia in the summer of 1776, the old brick city where the Declaration of Independence was written, adopted and first read aloud. A calm history for sleep.
Wander through the market sheds and the wharves, spend quiet evenings with Benjamin Franklin, John and Abigail Adams and a young Thomas Jefferson writing by candlelight, follow the hot July days in the State House gently and from up close, and walk through Independence Hall as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
