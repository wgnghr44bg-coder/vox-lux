Drift off to life on a cattle drive in 1870, riding up the Chisholm Trail from Texas to Abilene, Kansas, with three thousand longhorns, a crew of cowboys and a boy of fifteen on his very first drive. A calm history for sleep about the open range, the chuckwagon, the horses and the long nights under the prairie stars.
Gather the wild longhorns in the spring roundup, ride drag in the dust behind the herd, eat sourdough biscuits from the chuckwagon, sing softly to the cattle on night watch, swim the herd across the Red River, pay the toll in the Indian Territory, ride through a summer storm on the prairie, watch a great herd of buffalo pass, and finally reach the railway at Abilene.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
