Drift off to the story of how Viking families lived through the long, dark winters of the north, around eleven hundred years ago, in Scandinavia, Iceland and Greenland.
Visit a quiet farm from the last days of autumn to the first thaw of spring: the smoky longhouse, the wool and the fire, the stories, the old festivals and the long nights.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
and softly changing images.
