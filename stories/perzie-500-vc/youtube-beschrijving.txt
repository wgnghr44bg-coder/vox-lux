Drift off to the story of ancient Persia in 500 BC, the empire of Darius the Great and his palace city of Persepolis, the largest empire the world had yet seen. A calm history for sleep.
Climb the great staircase of Persepolis, watch the peoples of the empire bring their gifts at the New Year, ride the Royal Road with the king's messengers, rest in a paradise garden, meet the workers and weavers who built the palaces, and walk among the ruins as they stand today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
