Drift off to the story of life with the army of Alexander the Great in 331 BC, seen through the eyes of a young step counter from Macedonia, whose job was to measure every road the army walked. A calm history for sleep about a moving city of fifty thousand people, travelling through Egypt, Mesopotamia and Persia.
Count your steps along the Nile, help measure the first streets of Alexandria, cross the desert to the oracle of Siwa, sit by the campfires with the scholars, wade across the Tigris, watch the moon turn red during a real eclipse, enter Babylon through the blue Ishtar Gate, learn from the astronomers on the rooftops, and walk up the great stairs of Persepolis.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
