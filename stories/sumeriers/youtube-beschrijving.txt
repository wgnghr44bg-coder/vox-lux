Drift off to the story of the Sumerians, the first civilization, in the city of Ur around 2100 BC, when King Shulgi ruled from the great ziggurat of the moon god. A calm history for sleep.
Walk through the reed marshes and the mudbrick streets of Ur, climb toward the ziggurat, hear about King Shulgi's legendary run, spend a day with a trading family, see how writing began with clay tokens, sit in the tablet house, taste beer from the hymn to Ninkasi, sail to Dilmun and hear of faraway Meluhha, meet Enheduanna, the first writer known by name, listen to the stories of Gilgamesh and the great flood, play the Royal Game of Ur, and follow the archaeologists who brought Ur back to light.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
