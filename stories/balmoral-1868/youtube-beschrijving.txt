Drift off to an autumn at Balmoral in 1868, Queen Victoria's beloved castle in the Scottish Highlands, told gently through the eyes of a ghillie's family on the royal estate. A calm history for sleep about golden birch woods, purple heather, misty lochs and a queen who found peace in the hills.
Walk the hills with the ghillies, hear the red stags roar in the glens, read the queen's new Highland journal by the fire, picnic beside Loch Muick, cheer the tug of war at the Braemar Gathering, dance at the ghillies' ball, sing the psalms at Crathie church, welcome the queen into a little cottage, and watch the first snow settle on Lochnagar.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
