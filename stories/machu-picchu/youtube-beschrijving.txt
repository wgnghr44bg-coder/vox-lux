Drift off to the story of Machu Picchu and the Inca Empire around 1460, when the emperor Pachacuti ordered a royal estate on a ridge above the clouds, and thousands of people from all over the Andes came to build it. A calm history for sleep.
Discover who really built Machu Picchu, follow a young stonemason as he learns to fit granite without mortar, see the hidden drains and the terraces, visit golden Cusco and the Temple of the Sun, walk the royal road, run with the chasqui messengers, cross a bridge woven from grass, read the knotted quipus, meet the weavers of the finest cloth, greet the sun at Inti Raymi, and follow the lost city through the centuries to today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
