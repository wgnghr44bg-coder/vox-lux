At the heart of the empire is the city of Cusco.

Cusco lies in a high valley, more than three thousand metres above the sea. Mountains rise all around it. Two small rivers flow through it. They have been channelled between stone walls so they do not flood. And over them, the Inca have built a city of stone.

--

The heart of Cusco is a great square. It is called the Haukaypata, the place of joy and rest. It is huge. On feast days, thousands of people gather there. The floor of the square is covered with sand brought all the way from the sea. The sand of the ocean, in the middle of the mountains. A sign that the empire reaches from the mountains to the coast.

Around the square stand the palaces of the emperors. Each emperor builds his own palace. When he passes away, his palace stays. His family lives on in it. And his body, carefully preserved, still lives there too. Servants care for it. They dress it in fine clothes and offer it food and drink. The old rulers are still part of the city.

{img:cusco-street-inca-stone-wall-trapezoid-doorway-people-llamas}
The walls of Cusco are famous. They are built of huge stones fitted together without any mortar, the paste that holds bricks together. The stones fit so tightly that you cannot slide a knife between them. Some have twelve corners or more. And still they fit their neighbours perfectly. Doorways and windows are shaped like trapezoids. Wider at the bottom, narrower at the top. That makes them stronger in earthquakes.

--

Some people say Cusco is shaped like a puma, a great mountain cat. The city is its body. A river forms its tail. And on the hill above the city stands its head. A huge fortress called Sacsayhuamán. Its walls zigzag across the hill like the teeth of the puma. Some of its stones are taller than two people and weigh more than a hundred tons.

{img:sacsayhuaman-zigzag-giant-stone-walls-hill-above-cusco-clouds}
In 1460, Cusco is growing. Pachacuti is rebuilding it in stone. Workers come from all over the empire. Stone cutters, builders, carriers. The best stonemasons learn their skills here. And some of them will soon be sent on to a new project. Far away, down the valley, on a ridge in the clouds.
