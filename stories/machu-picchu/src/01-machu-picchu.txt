Tonight we go more than five hundred years back. We go high into the mountains of South America, to the land of the Inca. The year is around 1460. And on a narrow ridge between two mountain peaks, something remarkable is being built.

--

It is early morning. Clouds fill the deep valleys below like a white sea. The sharp green peaks rise out of them like islands. Far below, a river roars, but you cannot see it. Only hear it. And on the ridge, between the peaks, stand walls of grey stone. Houses and temples and stairways and steps of land, climbing up the mountainside. Some are finished. Some are still being built. Smoke rises from little fires. And people are already at work.

This place will later be called Machu Picchu. It means old peak, in the language of the Inca. It is one of the most beautiful places on earth. And for a long time, it was one of the most mysterious.

If you enjoy calm journeys into the past like this one, you are warmly welcome to subscribe to the channel, so you can find the next one easily. And now, let us begin.

--

{img:inca-stonemasons-shaping-granite-blocks-terraces-mountain-morning}
Who built Machu Picchu? How did they get the stones up a steep mountain? Why here, of all places, on a ridge above the clouds? And why was it forgotten for hundreds of years?

For a long time, people did not know. Some made up wild stories. But today, archaeologists know a great deal. They have studied the walls and the stones. They have read the old Spanish writings about the Inca. They have found the graves of people who lived there. And slowly, the answers have come.

{img:inca-empire-andes-mountains-roads-villages-terraces-vast-landscape}
Tonight we will travel through the Inca Empire. The biggest empire in the Americas. We will walk the great stone roads. We will cross a bridge made of grass. We will meet the runners who carry messages over the mountains. We will visit the golden city of Cusco. We will meet the great emperor who ordered Machu Picchu. And we will follow a young stonemason as he helps to build it.

There will be worries along the way. Can the stones be moved? Will the bridge hold? Will the emperor be pleased?

--

{img:llamas-grazing-high-andes-snow-peaks-shepherd-girl-morning}
So let us start high in the Andes, in the cold clear morning air. The sky is very blue. The mountains are white with snow at the top. Llamas graze on the slopes, with little ribbons in their ears. And somewhere a shepherd girl is playing a flute made of bone. The notes float out across the valley.
