Drift off to the story of Joan of Arc, the peasant girl from the village of Domrémy who heard voices, rode to the uncrowned king and helped to save France in 1429, told gently and calmly, beginning with the autumn harvest in her quiet village. A calm history for sleep about faith, courage, a white banner and the long road from the cow meadows to the cathedral of Reims.
Bring in the autumn harvest by the river Meuse, dance around the old Ladies' Tree, ride through winter nights to Chinon, find the hidden Dauphin among his courtiers, answer the learned men of Poitiers, enter besieged Orléans by torchlight, watch the siege lifted from the city walls, stand beside the new king in the cathedral of Reims, and follow how her name was cleared and honoured.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
