Drift off to life among the Celts and the druids of ancient Gaul in 60 BC, just before the Romans came, in the hilltop town of Bibracte. A calm history for sleep about a blacksmith's family, a boy with a wonderful memory who is chosen to study with the druids, and the real druid Diviciacus, who once dined with Cicero in Rome.
Walk through the town on its high wooded hill, watch the smith forge iron and red enamel, learn the long verses by heart under the old oak, name the stars on the hilltop at night, see the mistletoe cut with a golden sickle, visit the sacred springs and groves, feast in the great hall while the bard sings, travel to the great assembly of the druids in the forest, and light the fires of Beltane and Samhain.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
