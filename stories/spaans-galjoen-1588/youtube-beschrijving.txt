Drift off to the story of life on a Spanish galleon in 1588, the year of the great Armada, from the harbour of Lisbon to the grey seas around Scotland and Ireland. A calm history for sleep about everyday life at sea.
Sail with a twelve-year-old ship's boy on his first voyage, and meet the old sailors, the cook, the pilot and the friar. Learn how they ate, slept and found their way by the sun and the stars, hear their songs and prayers at dusk, watch the northern lights, and come home at last to the green coast of Spain.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
