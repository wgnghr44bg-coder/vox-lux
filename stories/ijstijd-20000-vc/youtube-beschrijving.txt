Drift off to a calm history of the Ice Age, around 20,000 years ago, when great ice sheets covered the north and people survived on the frozen steppe of Europe.
Visit rock shelters and mammoth-bone houses, see how fur clothing was sewn with bone needles, follow the reindeer hunters through the long winter, meet the mammoths and cave lions, and step into the painted caves of Lascaux and Chauvet, until the ice slowly melts away.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud sounds, no jump scares: just a gentle, slow voice, soft and relaxing 432 Hz background music and the quiet crackle of a warm fire, with softly changing images in the glow of the fire and gently drifting embers. Let the story carry you gently into sleep.
