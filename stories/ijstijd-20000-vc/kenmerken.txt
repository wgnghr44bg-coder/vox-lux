# trefwoord | hoe archeologen het reconstrueren
mammoth | a woolly mammoth: shaggy long reddish-brown hair, a high domed head, a sloping back, small ears, long curved spiral tusks, about the size of an African elephant
cave | a natural limestone rock shelter or cave mouth used as a seasonal camp, hide windbreaks and a hearth near the entrance, no carved stone architecture
spear | a long wooden spear with a stone or bone point bound with sinew, sometimes thrown with a spear-thrower, no metal
