Drift off to the story of Napoleon's Grande Armée in 1812, the great march to Moscow in the summer and the long road back through the Russian winter. A calm history for sleep.
March beside the young soldiers through the dust of Lithuania, sit at the bivouac fires, write letters home, enter the empty city of Moscow, follow the first snow and the crossing of the Berezina gently and from a distance, and hear how friendship and small kindnesses carried people through, then walk the road of 1812 as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
