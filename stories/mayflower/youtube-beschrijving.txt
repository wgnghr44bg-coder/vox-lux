Drift off to the story of the Mayflower, the small merchant ship that carried 102 passengers from Plymouth in England across the Atlantic to Cape Cod in the autumn of 1620. A calm history for sleep.
Begin in the quiet village of Scrooby and the canals of Leiden, live between the decks through the long grey weeks at sea, and follow the first winter, the spring of 1621 and the first harvest at Plymouth, gently and without frightening details.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
and softly changing images.
