Tonight we are going four hundred years back, to the autumn of 1620.

[pause]

A small wooden ship is waiting in the harbour of Plymouth, on the south coast of England.

[pause]

Her name is the Mayflower.

[long pause]

She is not a grand ship. She is an old cargo ship, used to carrying wine across the Channel, and her timbers still smell faintly of it.

[pause]

On board are one hundred and two passengers, with their chests and their blankets, their tools, and a few chickens in wooden cages.

[pause]

They are about to cross the whole Atlantic Ocean, in a season when no sensible sailor would.

[long pause]

So settle in, and get comfortable.

[pause]

The wind is turning, and the voyage is about to begin.
