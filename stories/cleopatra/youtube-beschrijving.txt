Drift off to the story of ancient Egypt in the time of Cleopatra, the last pharaoh, in her city of Alexandria and along the Nile in 31 BC. A calm history for sleep.
Walk the marble streets of Alexandria, climb toward the great lighthouse, read in the famous Library, spend a day with a family of glassmakers, sail up the Nile past the pyramids and the temples, meet Caesar and Antony, follow the last year of the queen gently and from a distance, and visit Alexandria as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
