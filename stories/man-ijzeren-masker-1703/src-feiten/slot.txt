So we leave the old fortress on the eastern edge of Paris.

The lamps along the ramparts burn low, and the guards walk their slow rounds beneath the towers. Far to the south, the sea washes softly against the rocks of Sainte-Marguerite, below a window with three rows of bars. The secret of the masked prisoner rests where he left it, quiet and unspoken.

Should sleep still be a little way off, another calm story from long ago is waiting on the screen.

Sleep well, and good night.
