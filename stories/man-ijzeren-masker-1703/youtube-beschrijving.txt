Drift off to the calm, true history of the Man in the Iron Mask, the secret prisoner of King Louis XIV who died in the Bastille in 1703.
Follow his long journey from an arrest at Dunkirk in 1669 to the mountain fortresses of Pignerol and Exilles, the island of Sainte-Marguerite and the Bastille in Paris, with his faithful jailer Saint-Mars, Fouquet, Lauzun, Voltaire and the many guesses about who he really was.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
