Drift off to the story of the journey of Apollo 11 in July 1969, when three astronauts flew to the moon and two of them walked on it. A calm history for sleep, for the anniversary of the moon landing.
Meet Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin and Michael Collins, and a family in Houston whose father works at Mission Control. Watch the giant Saturn V rise from the coast of Florida, float with the astronauts across the quiet space between the Earth and the moon, follow the landing of the Eagle and the first step, circle the moon alone with Columbia, and come home at last to the blue Pacific.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
