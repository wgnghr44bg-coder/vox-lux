Drift off to the story of Troy in its last days, the windy city of King Priam, Hector and Helen, as the old poets sang it, and as the archaeologists found it. A calm history for sleep.
Walk the great walls and the lanes of the lower town, spend ordinary days with the weavers and the horse tamers, listen to Hector and Andromache on the tower, follow the wooden horse and the last night gently and from a distance, sail home with Odysseus, and visit the hill of Troy as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
