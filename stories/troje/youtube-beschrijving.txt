Drift off to the calm story of the fall of Troy, as told by Homer and uncovered by archaeology, around 1200 BC.
Walk the sloping walls of the real city, meet Priam, Hector, Helen and Achilles, follow Odysseus on his long voyage home, and see how Schliemann and the archaeologists after him searched for the Troy of the poems.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
