Drift off to the story of life in Paris in the summer of 1789, the summer of the Bastille, seen through the eyes of an ordinary family. A calm history for sleep, for Bastille Day.
Meet a carpenter's family in the Faubourg Saint-Antoine, stand in the bread queues at dawn, walk to the market of Les Halles and along the Seine, listen to the news from Versailles in the cafés of the Palais-Royal and in a little printing shop, follow the warm days of July gently from the streets nearby, and see how Paris celebrated afterwards.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
