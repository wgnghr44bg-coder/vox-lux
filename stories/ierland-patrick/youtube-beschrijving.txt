Drift off to the story of Ireland in the time of Saint Patrick, in 432 AD, when a bishop came back to the green island where he had once been a slave. A calm history for sleep.
Spend a day in a round ringfort among the cattle and the soft rain, sit by the fire with the poets and storytellers, learn the old laws and the four great festivals, follow Patrick along the old roads to the Easter fire on the hill of Slane, and visit the holy wells, ringforts and mountains of Ireland as they are today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
