Drift off to the story of the last days of World War II in Europe, in May 1945, when the long war ended and people everywhere came out into the streets, the sunshine and the lights. A calm history for sleep.
Watch food fall from the sky over the hungry Netherlands, see two armies shake hands on the river Elbe, light candles in the windows of Denmark, sit in a little red schoolhouse in Reims at two in the morning, and spend VE Day in London with the crowds, the princesses, the street parties, the church bells and the bonfires, and then follow the soldiers home, all told gently, without battles.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
