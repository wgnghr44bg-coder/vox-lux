And then it is New Year's morning. The first day of the first month.

Everyone gets up very early, before dawn. The family washes and dresses in their best clothes. The children have new clothes if possible. The daughter wears a new silk jacket, sewn by her mother. The eldest son has a new belt. The little boy has new cloth shoes. He keeps looking down at them.

--

First, the family honours the ancestors. In the main room there is a small altar. On it stand wooden tablets with the names of the ancestors. Grandfather's father and grandfather's grandfather, and those before them. The family lays out food and wine before the tablets. The grandfather lights the incense. And everyone bows deeply, all the way to the floor. They tell the ancestors that a new year has begun. And they ask them to watch over the family.

Then comes the most important part of the morning. The children and grandchildren greet the grandparents. The grandparents sit side by side on their mats. One by one, starting with the oldest son, everyone kneels before them and bows. They wish them a long life. And the grandparents bless them in return.

{img:han-children-kneeling-bowing-to-grandparents-new-year-greeting}
Then the family drinks the New Year wine. It is a special wine, mixed with pepper and the leaves of a certain tree. People call it pepper wine. They believe it brings a long life and keeps sickness away.

--

But there is a funny rule about who drinks first. Usually in China, the oldest person always goes first. But with the New Year wine, it is the other way around. The youngest drinks first. And the oldest drinks last.

Why? An old Han writer explains it. The young are gaining a year. So we congratulate them first. The old are losing a year. So they drink last. It is a gentle joke about growing old. The grandfather pretends to be sad about it every year. Everyone laughs.

{img:little-boy-sipping-small-cup-pepper-wine-family-smiling-morning}
So the little boy of four drinks first. He takes a tiny sip from a little cup. His face screws up. It is spicy and strange. Everyone laughs. Then his sister drinks, and his brother. Then the mother and father. And last of all, the grandmother and the grandfather. The grandfather lifts his cup high. To another year, he says. And he drinks it all in one go.

--

Outside, the sun is coming up. Its first light falls on the courtyard and the peach boards on the gate. The new year has begun. And so far, everything has gone well.
