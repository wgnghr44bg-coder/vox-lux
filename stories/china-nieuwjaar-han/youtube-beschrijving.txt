Drift off to the story of Chinese New Year in ancient China, in the Han dynasty capital of Luoyang around 150 AD, when families cleaned their houses, honoured the stove god and the ancestors, and stayed up all night to see the old year out. A calm history for sleep.
Walk through snowy Luoyang and its great market, visit the emperor's palace, learn how the Han calendar set the date of the New Year, spend the last weeks of the year with a family in their courtyard house, watch the masked parade of the Great Nuo, listen to bamboo popping in the fire, hang peach wood door guardians, drink the pepper wine on New Year's morning, hear how the twelve animals got their order, and walk under the first full moon with little clay lamps.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
