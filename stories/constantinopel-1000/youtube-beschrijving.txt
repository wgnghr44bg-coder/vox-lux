Drift off to the story of life in Constantinople around the year 1000, the Queen of Cities between Europe and Asia. A calm history for sleep about bakers, markets, golden churches and chariot races.
Meet a family of bakers near the great street of the Mese, walk through the markets of silk and spices, stand under the golden dome of Hagia Sophia, watch an icon painter at work, see the emperor's throne with its singing birds, hear the story of a guardsman from the far north, cheer at the Hippodrome, and celebrate Easter at midnight with candles and bells.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
