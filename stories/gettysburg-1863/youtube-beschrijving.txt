Drift off to a small Pennsylvania town in the summer of 1863, and discover what happened to the families of Gettysburg before, during and after the great battle. A calm history for sleep.
Walk Baltimore Street with fifteen-year-old Tillie Pierce, visit the carriage shops, the college and the cemetery gatehouse, follow the two armies along the country roads gently and from a distance, see how the whole town cared for the wounded, and stand on the hill in November when President Lincoln spoke his few famous words, all the way to Gettysburg today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
