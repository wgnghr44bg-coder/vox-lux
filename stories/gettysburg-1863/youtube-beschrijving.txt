Drift off to the story of Gettysburg, the quiet Pennsylvania town where, in July 1863, the greatest battle of the Civil War was fought, and where Abraham Lincoln gave the Gettysburg Address. A calm history for sleep.
Spend the summer with fifteen-year-old Tillie Pierce, the cemetery keeper Elizabeth Thorn and the townspeople of Gettysburg, follow the three days of July gently and from a distance, and stand on the hill in November as President Lincoln speaks his few famous words.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
