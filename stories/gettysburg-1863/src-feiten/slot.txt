On Baltimore Street, the old brick houses stand quiet in the dark. The cupola on Seminary Ridge is only a pale shape against the night. Up on the hill, the gatehouse arch is still, and the curved rows lie peacefully under the old trees. Out in the fields, the wheat bends softly in the warm wind. A deer steps out from the woods, and the fireflies blink slowly above the grass.
