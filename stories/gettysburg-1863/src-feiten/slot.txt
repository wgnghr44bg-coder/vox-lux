We have walked through Gettysburg in 1863, from its orchards and brick houses to the long days of July and the quiet November afternoon on Cemetery Hill.

Now the lamps along Baltimore Street go dark. The ridges fade into the night, and the curved rows rest beneath the old trees. Nothing from this story has to stay with you.

Whoever is still awake will find another quiet story from long ago there on the screen. Pressing subscribe is a small help to this little channel.

Rest now, as the cool night air settles over the fields of Pennsylvania.

Sleep well, and good night.
