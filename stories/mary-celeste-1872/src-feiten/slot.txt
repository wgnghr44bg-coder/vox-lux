So we leave the little brigantine rocking at anchor in a quiet harbour.

Her sails are furled, and the lamps in her cabin are dark. The long swell of the Atlantic rolls past, slow and grey, as it did on that winter afternoon so long ago. Somewhere far out on the water, the ten travellers keep their secret, and the sea keeps it with them.

For anyone still listening, another slow story from the past is resting on the screen, ready whenever it is wanted.

Sleep well, and good night.
