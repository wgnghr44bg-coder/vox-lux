Drift off to the calm, true history of the Mary Celeste, the ship found drifting in the Atlantic in December 1872 with not one of her ten people on board.
Follow her from a shipyard in Nova Scotia and the busy port of New York to the Azores, the court in Gibraltar and the reef off Haiti, with Captain Briggs and his family, the crew of the Dei Gratia, the theories of what happened and the legend made by Arthur Conan Doyle.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
