Drift off to the story of building a medieval cathedral, on the great building site of Salisbury Cathedral in the year 1250, seen through the eyes of a stonemason's son who begins his apprenticeship. A calm history for sleep about patience, skill and faith, and the thousands of hands that built a church of stone to last for centuries.
Leave the windy hill of Old Sarum for the green valley of five rivers, ride to the quarry to choose stone that rings like a bell, learn the secret of the pointed arch from the master mason, carve curling leaves in the masons' lodge, walk inside the great treadwheel crane, polish dark Purbeck marble by the sea, watch the glaziers make silvery windows, see Magna Carta in the treasury, and stand in the crowd as the king comes for the consecration.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
