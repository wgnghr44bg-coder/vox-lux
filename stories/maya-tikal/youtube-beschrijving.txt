Drift off to the story of the Maya city of Tikal in 750 AD, when its red temples rose above the rainforest of Guatemala and its kings ruled one of the greatest cities of the ancient Americas. A calm history for sleep.
Walk across the Great Plaza between the Temple of the Great Jaguar and the Temple of the Masks, visit the palace and the builders, spend a day with a farming family grinding corn and weaving, taste chocolate the Maya way, learn about glyphs, the Long Count calendar and the stars, watch the ballgame, listen to the rain over the reservoirs, and visit Tikal as it is today, with howler monkeys calling over the ruins.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
