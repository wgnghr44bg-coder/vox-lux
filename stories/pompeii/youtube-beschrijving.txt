Drift off to the story of Pompeii, the Roman town beneath Mount Vesuvius, on its last day in 79 AD.
Wander through painted houses, rose gardens and busy streets, follow Pliny the Elder and Pliny the Younger across the bay through the long night of ash, and see how the town was forgotten and found again.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice and softly changing images.
