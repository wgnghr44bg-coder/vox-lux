Close your eyes and drift back almost two thousand years, to a small Roman town on the edge of a sparkling bay.

In this calm sleep documentary we spend the last day of Pompeii together: the painted houses and rose gardens, the bakeries and busy streets, the friends Pliny the Elder and Pliny the Younger across the bay, the long night of ash, and how the town was forgotten and found again. Nothing frightening, no graphic details. Just a slow, gentle story, told softly, with quiet images and drifting mist.

Put on your headphones, dim the lights and let the story carry you to sleep.
