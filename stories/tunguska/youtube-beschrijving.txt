Drift off to the story of the Tunguska Event, the huge explosion in the sky over the Siberian forest on the morning of 30 June 1908.
Walk through the great taiga with the Evenki reindeer herders, feel the sky flash over the small village of Vanavara, and follow the scientists who came years later to read the story in the fallen trees, gently and without frightening details.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
and softly changing images.
