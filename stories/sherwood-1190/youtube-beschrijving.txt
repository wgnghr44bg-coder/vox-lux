Drift off to the story of life in Sherwood Forest around the year 1190, in the time of King Richard the Lionheart, and to the old legend of Robin Hood. A calm history for sleep about a charcoal burner's family in a forest village, the king's deer and the king's foresters, and the stories that would make Sherwood famous all over the world.
Watch the charcoal mound smoke through the night, take the pigs into the autumn oak woods, learn to read deer tracks from an old forester, visit the market and the castle of Nottingham, walk in the herb garden of the white monks, practise archery on the village green, dance around the maypole, and listen by the fire to the tales of Little John, Friar Tuck and the silver arrow.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
