Drift off to the story of Captain Scott's last expedition to Antarctica, from 1910 to 1913, and how his men lived through a winter at the bottom of the world. A calm history for sleep.
Sail south on the old whaling ship Terra Nova, spend the long polar night in the wooden hut at Cape Evans with Wilson, Bowers, Oates and Cherry-Garrard, meet the ponies, the dogs and the penguins, and follow the journey to the South Pole gently and from a distance, to the quiet hut that still stands beneath Mount Erebus today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
