Drift off to the story of Captain Scott's last expedition to Antarctica, 1910 to 1913, and the race to the South Pole in 1912. A calm history for sleep.
Sail south on the old ship Terra Nova, spend the long polar night in the wooden hut at Cape Evans with Wilson, Bowers, Oates and Cherry-Garrard, walk with the ponies and the dogs across the ice, and follow the journey to the Pole and back gently and from a distance, to the quiet hut that still stands beneath Mount Erebus today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
