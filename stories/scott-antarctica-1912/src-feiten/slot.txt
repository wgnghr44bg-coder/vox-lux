So we leave the old hut on its cape of black sand.

The long table is still, and the stove has gone cold. Outside, the snow lies quiet across the frozen bay. High above Erebus, the southern lights ripple, green and soft and slow. Down by the shore, a seal breathes once at its hole in the ice and sinks back into the dark. The wind has dropped, and nothing moves on the white plain.

For those still awake, another gentle story from the far corners of the past is waiting on the screen.

Sleep well, and good night.
