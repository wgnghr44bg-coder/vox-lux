So we leave the old hut on its cape of black sand.

The long table is still, and the stove has gone cold. Outside, the snow lies quiet across the frozen bay. High above Erebus, the southern lights begin to ripple, green and soft and slow. Down by the shore, a seal breathes once at its hole in the ice, and sinks back into the dark. The wind has dropped. Nothing moves on the white plain.

Should your eyes still be open, there is another quiet story from long ago, waiting for you on the screen. And if this voyage brought you some rest, a subscription keeps the next one close at hand.

Let the cold and the distance stay far away now. You are warm, and there is nowhere left to walk.

Sleep well, and good night.
