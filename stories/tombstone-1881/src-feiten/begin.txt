This is Tombstone, a silver town on a high desert mesa in Arizona.

Most of us know Tombstone from one famous afternoon and thirty seconds of gunfire. But the people who lived there knew a very different town. A town of miners and cooks, newspaper men and schoolteachers, of oysters in the desert and roses in the courtyards. So what was life really like in Tombstone, on all the ordinary days?
