Drift off with 90 quiet facts about Tombstone, Arizona, the silver town of Wyatt Earp and Doc Holliday, from the day a lonely prospector found silver in 1877 to the famous afternoon of October 1881 and the quiet town that still stands today.
Meet the miners and their candles, Nellie Cashman and her kitchen, the diarist George Parsons, the photographer C. S. Fly, the ranchers of the valleys and the lawmen of a town too tough to die. Nothing to follow and nothing to remember.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
