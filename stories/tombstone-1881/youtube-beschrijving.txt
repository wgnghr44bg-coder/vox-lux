Drift off to the story of Tombstone, Arizona, the silver town of Wyatt Earp and Doc Holliday, in the weeks before the Gunfight at the O.K. Corral in October 1881. A calm history for sleep.
Walk the boardwalks of Allen Street with the miners, Nellie Cashman and the diarist George Parsons, meet the Earp brothers, the ranchers of the San Pedro Valley and Doc Holliday, and follow that cold, windy day on Fremont Street slowly and from a distance, to the quiet town that still stands today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
