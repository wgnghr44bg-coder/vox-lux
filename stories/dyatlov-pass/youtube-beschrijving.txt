Drift off to the story of nine young friends and a winter hike in the Ural Mountains of Russia in 1959, a calm history for sleep documentary about the Dyatlov Pass mystery.
Follow the group from the snowy streets of Sverdlovsk, through the forest and up onto the slope of Kholat Syakhl, then walk through the search, the investigation and the theories that people still discuss today, told slowly and without gruesome detail.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
and softly changing images.
