Drift off to the story of the Silk Road with Marco Polo, who left Venice in 1271 at seventeen and travelled across Asia to the court of Kublai Khan. A calm history for sleep.
Ride with the camel caravans from Armenia and Persia over the roof of the world, rest in caravanserais and Mongol gers, cross the singing sands of the desert of Lop, visit Xanadu, Khanbaliq and Hangzhou, sail home past Sumatra and India, and follow the old road as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
