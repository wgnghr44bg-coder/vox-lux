Babylon lies in Mesopotamia, which means, in Greek, the land between the rivers.

The two rivers are the Tigris, and the Euphrates. They rise in the mountains of Turkey, far to the north, and flow south, for more than a thousand kilometres, across the plains, coming close together, near Babylon, and then flowing on, to the Persian Gulf. Today, most of this land is in the country of Iraq.

--

It is a flat land, of rich, brown soil, brought down by the rivers, over thousands of years, and laid down, in their floods. But it hardly ever rains. The summers are long, and very hot, and dry. And so, without the rivers, nothing would grow.

But with the rivers, everything grows. For thousands of years, the people of Mesopotamia have dug canals, to carry the river water out, across the land, to the fields, and the gardens, and the palm groves. And so, along the rivers, and the canals, the land is green, and rich, with fields of barley, and wheat, and sesame, and gardens of vegetables, and great forests of date palms.

{img:ancient-sumerian-city-ruins-ziggurat-ur-reed-marsh-boats}
This is one of the places where civilisation began.

--

More than five thousand years ago, here, in the south of Mesopotamia, the Sumerians built the first cities in the world: Ur, and Uruk, and Eridu, and Lagash, with their temples, and their walls, and their kings. Here, they invented writing, the first writing in the world, pressed into clay, with a reed. Here, they invented the wheel, and the plough, and the sailboat, and mathematics, and law, and the first written stories, and poems, like the great Epic of Gilgamesh.

By the year 600 BC, all of this is already ancient history. The Sumerians are long gone. Their cities are old, and some are ruined. But the Babylonians remember them, and honour them, and copy their writings, and their prayers, and still use their ancient language, in the temples, as we might use Latin.

{img:reed-marshes-southern-mesopotamia-reed-houses-canoe-water-buffalo}
In the south, near the sea, there are great marshes, of reeds, and water, where the people live in houses made of reeds, and travel in canoes, and fish, and keep water buffalo, as they have for thousands of years.

--

And in the middle of this ancient land, on the river Euphrates, there is Babylon: a city that has been here for more than fifteen hundred years. More than a thousand years before, it was the capital of the great king Hammurabi, who made one of the first great collections of laws, in the world. Since then, it has been conquered, and destroyed, and rebuilt, again, and again.

And now, in the year 600 BC, a new dynasty of kings has made it great once more, greater than it has ever been.
