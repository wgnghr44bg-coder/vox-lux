Tonight we go more than two and a half thousand years back. We go to the great city of Babylon, around the year 600 before Christ.

--

It is early morning on a wide flat plain. The sun rises out of the brown land in the east. Its first light falls on a great river, the Euphrates. It is wide and slow and brown. It flows between banks of reeds and groves of date palms. And beside the river, out of the morning haze, rises a city.

It is the biggest city in the world. People say its walls are so thick that a chariot with four horses can drive along the top and turn round. Behind them are temples and palaces and houses and gardens. And above them all rises a great stepped tower. Level after level, it climbs toward the sky, with a temple at the very top.

If you enjoy calm journeys into the past like this one, you are warmly welcome to subscribe to the channel, so you can find the next one easily. And now, let us begin.

--

This is Babylon.

{img:ishtar-gate-blue-glazed-bricks-golden-lions-dragons-morning-light}
Its name is still famous today. It is in the Bible and in old stories and songs. The Tower of Babel. The Hanging Gardens. The rivers of Babylon. And yet, for a very long time, Babylon was only a name. A mound of earth and broken bricks in the desert.

In the year 600 before Christ, it is at the height of its glory. It is the capital of a great empire. It reaches from the sea in the south to the mountains in the north. It reaches west all the way to the Mediterranean Sea and the border of Egypt. And it is ruled by one of the most famous kings of the ancient world. His name is Nebuchadnezzar. But even a great empire can have enemies watching from the edges.

--

Tonight we will walk through Babylon. We will pass through the great blue gate with its golden lions and dragons. We will walk along the holy road. We will climb toward the great tower and visit the temple of the god Marduk. We will spend a day with a family in their mud brick house and sleep on the flat roof under the stars. We will sit in a school where boys learn to write on clay. We will watch the star watchers on their towers at night. And we will join the great festival of the New Year.

{img:babylon-street-mudbrick-houses-people-donkeys-morning-market}
So let us walk together along the river bank, in the cool of the early morning. Among the palm trees and the reeds and the fishing boats. Toward the great walls and the gates of Babylon.
