We have walked through the gates of Babylon, past its blue lions and its great tower, into its houses, schools and temples. We have seen how a city of mud brick became the wonder of its age.

Now the lamps along the river go out, one by one. The tower fades into the dark, and the water moves on towards the sea. There is no need to remember any of it.

Should sleep still be far away, another gentle journey into history is ready for you on the screen. And if you enjoyed this one, you are welcome to subscribe.

Let your breath grow slow and easy, and let the old city drift back into the past.

Sleep well, and good night.
