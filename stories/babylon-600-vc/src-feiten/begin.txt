Around the year 600 BC, one city stood above all others in the ancient world. Its name was Babylon. It rose from a flat plain beside the Euphrates, with blue gates, a great stepped tower and streets full of traders from distant lands. Yet behind its famous walls lived ordinary families, scribes, brewers and star watchers. In this story, we will look at how the city worked, and why it was remembered for so long.
