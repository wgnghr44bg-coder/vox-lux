Drift off to the story of ancient Babylon around 600 BC, the greatest city in the world, in the reign of King Nebuchadnezzar, beside the river Euphrates. A calm history for sleep.
Walk through the blue Ishtar Gate with its golden lions, bulls and dragons, look up at the great ziggurat, visit the temple of Marduk, spend a day with a family who sleep on their roof under the stars, learn to write on clay with the young scribes, count in sixties, watch the astronomers on the temple roofs, join the New Year festival, wonder about the Hanging Gardens, and visit Babylon as it is today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
