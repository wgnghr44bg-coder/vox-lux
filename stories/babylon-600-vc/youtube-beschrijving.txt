Drift off to the story of ancient Babylon around 600 BC, the greatest city in the world in the days of King Nebuchadnezzar, beside the river Euphrates. A calm history for sleep.
Walk through the blue Ishtar Gate with its golden bulls and dragons, look up at the great tower, spend an evening with a family who sleep on their roof under the stars, learn to write on clay with the young scribes, count in sixties, watch the astronomers on the temple roofs, join the New Year festival, wonder about the Hanging Gardens, and see how Babylon was found again.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
