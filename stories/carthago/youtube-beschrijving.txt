Drift off to the story of life in ancient Carthage, around 180 BC, the great Phoenician city by the sea on the coast of North Africa. A calm history for sleep about shipbuilders, traders, purple dye and old stories.
Meet a shipwright's family near the famous round harbour, hear the story of Queen Elissa and the oxhide, learn how the Phoenicians gave the world the alphabet, see how purple dye was made from sea snails, visit the markets and the farms, follow Hanno the Navigator down the coast of Africa, wait for a ship that sailed beyond the Pillars of Heracles, and remember the elephants of Carthage.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
