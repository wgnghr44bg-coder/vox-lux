Drift off to life in a Lancashire cotton town in 1840, at the height of the Industrial Revolution, told gently through the eyes of a family of spinners and weavers in Rochdale. A calm history for sleep about clattering clogs, tall chimneys, warm kitchens and the quiet courage of ordinary working people.
Wake to the tap of the knocker-up, walk to the mill in the dark, watch the great steam engine come to life, follow the spinning mules out and back, talk without sound in the weaving shed, shop at the Saturday night market, learn to read at the Sunday school, post a letter with the brand new Penny Black, cheer the Rushbearing carts at the Wakes, and climb the moors to Blackstone Edge.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
