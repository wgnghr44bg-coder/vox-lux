Drift off to the story of Japan in the year 1600, the age of the samurai, when Tokugawa Ieyasu, a Dutch ship and the battle of Sekigahara ended a century of war. A calm history for sleep.
Spend quiet days with a samurai family, walk through castle towns, rice fields and temples, sit in a tea room by the singing kettle, meet the English pilot William Adams, follow the great battle gently and from a distance, and see the long peace that came after.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
