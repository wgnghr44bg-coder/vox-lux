Drift off to the calm, true history of daily life in Japan in the age of the samurai, in the year 1600.
Follow a samurai through his day, walk through castle towns, rice villages, markets and quiet tea rooms, meet the first European visitors, and see how the Battle of Sekigahara brought the long age of war to an end.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
