So we leave Japan in the last days of 1600, as the first snow settles on the castle roofs.

The battle is over, and the roads are quiet again. In the villages, the rice is stored in the barns, and the farmers sit by their hearths. In the castle towns, the samurai lay their armour aside, and the lanterns glow behind the paper doors. Far away in the east, the little town of Edo sleeps beside its bay, not yet knowing that it will become one of the great cities of the world.

Before sleep finally comes, another story from an older world already sits on the screen, with lanterns and quiet roads of its own. Subscribing keeps these slow evenings coming.

Sleep well, and good night.
