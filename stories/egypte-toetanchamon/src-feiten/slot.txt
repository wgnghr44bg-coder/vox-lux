We have travelled along the Nile in the age of Tutankhamun, from its fields and markets to its temples, its festivals and the quiet valley where the young king was laid to rest.

Now the river darkens, and the lamps in the villages go out one by one. The western cliffs stand silent under the stars. The names and dates can simply drift away.

For those not yet asleep, another unhurried story from the ancient world is waiting on the screen. Subscribing helps this small channel grow.

Let the slow river carry the last thoughts of the day away.

Sleep well, and good night.
