Drift off to the story of ancient Egypt in the time of Tutankhamun, the young pharaoh who died in 1323 BC, told as a calm history for sleep.
Spend quiet days with the farmers along the Nile, the tomb-makers of the Place of Truth and the scribes of Thebes, meet the boy king and his queen Ankhesenamun, follow his funeral into the Valley of the Kings, and stand beside Howard Carter in 1922 as he sees "wonderful things".

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud sounds, no jump scares: just a gentle, slow voice, soft and relaxing 432 Hz background music and the quiet crackle of a warm fire, with softly changing images in the glow of the fire and gently drifting embers. Let the story carry you gently into sleep.
