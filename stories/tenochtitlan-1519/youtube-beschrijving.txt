Drift off to the story of Tenochtitlan in 1519, the Aztec capital built on an island in a shining lake, in the last months before Hernán Cortés arrived. A calm history for sleep.
Paddle along the canals and floating gardens, wander through the great market of Tlatelolco, spend an ordinary day with a Mexica family, visit the court of Moctezuma, listen to the poems of flower and song, follow the meeting of two worlds gently and from a distance, and see what remains in Mexico City today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
