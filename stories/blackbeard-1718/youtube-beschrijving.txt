Drift off to the story of Blackbeard and the Queen Anne's Revenge, in 1718, at the height of the golden age of piracy in the Caribbean and along the American coast. A calm history for sleep.
Visit the pirate haven of Nassau, sail through the warm islands, share the food, the rules and the long days of a pirate crew, follow Blackbeard to Charleston and the sandy Outer Banks, hear his last morning told gently and from a distance, and see the wreck of his ship and the island of Ocracoke as they are today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
