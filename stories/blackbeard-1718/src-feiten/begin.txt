In 1718, the warm waters of the Caribbean and the coast of the American colonies were crowded with ships carrying sugar, silver, rum and tobacco. For a few short years, they were also the hunting ground of pirates. This story follows the most famous of them, Edward Thatch, known as Blackbeard: the harbour of Nassau, the great ship called the Queen Anne's Revenge, daily life and food aboard a pirate crew, the blockade of Charleston, and the quiet sounds of North Carolina. It ends on the island of Ocracoke today, where his name still lingers.
