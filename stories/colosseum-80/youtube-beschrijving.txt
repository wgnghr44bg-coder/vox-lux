Drift off to the story of the Roman Colosseum in 80 AD, the year the emperor Titus opened the great amphitheatre with a hundred days of games, and the lives of the gladiators who trained beside it. A calm history for sleep.
Watch the builders and the sailors who raise the great awning, spend a day in the gladiator school with its barley porridge and wooden swords, meet the animal keepers, sit high in the seats with a baker and his son, hear how Priscus and Verus both won their freedom, and walk around the Colosseum as it stands today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
