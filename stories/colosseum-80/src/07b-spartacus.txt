The most famous gladiator in history lived a hundred and fifty years before the Colosseum. His name was Spartacus.

He came from the mountains of what is now Bulgaria. The Roman writers say he served in the Roman army, then ran away, was captured and sold as a slave. He was sent to a gladiator school near Naples.

--

One day, Spartacus and about seventy fellow gladiators escaped from the school, armed with knives and spits from the kitchen. On the road they found wagons carrying gladiators' weapons, and took them. Then they climbed up the slopes of Mount Vesuvius, which was then a quiet, green, wooded mountain, and camped there.

Thousands of slaves and poor farm workers ran away from the great estates of southern Italy and joined them. Spartacus became the leader of a great army, perhaps seventy thousand people or more.

{img:vesuvius-wooded-slopes-camp-escaped-slaves-vines-ropes-legend}
The Roman army came and blocked the only path down the mountain. They thought Spartacus was trapped. But his followers made ropes and ladders from the wild vines growing on the mountain. At night they climbed down the cliffs on the other side, and surprised the Romans from behind.

--

For two years, Spartacus and his army marched up and down Italy, beating one Roman army after another. Even the Roman writers, who hated him, admired him. One says he was not only brave and strong, but also clever and gentle.

It seems he wanted to lead his followers out of Italy, over the Alps, so they could go home to their own countries. But many of them wanted to stay and keep fighting.

{img:mountain-pass-alps-distant-army-turning-back-snow-peaks}
In the end, Spartacus was defeated in the south of Italy, and fell in battle. His body was never found. We will not describe what happened to his followers.

--

But his name was never forgotten. Two thousand years later, Spartacus became a hero and a symbol of the struggle for freedom. There are books about him, a famous ballet, and a famous film. At the end of the film, when the Romans ask the captured slaves which one of them is Spartacus, they all stand up, one after another, and say: I am Spartacus.
