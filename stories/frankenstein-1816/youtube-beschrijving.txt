Drift off to the rainy summer of 1816 on the shores of Lake Geneva, when a young Mary Shelley, Percy Shelley and Lord Byron sat by the fire at the Villa Diodati and told ghost stories, told gently through the eyes of a local boatman's family. A calm, softly atmospheric history for sleep about storms over the lake, candlelit conversations and the night a famous story was born.
Learn how a volcano on the other side of the world took away the summer, sail on the lake at dusk with the young poets, watch the lightning from the villa balcony, listen to the old German ghost stories by candlelight, share Mary's waking dream, walk through the market of old Geneva, visit the dungeon of Chillon and the great glacier below Mont Blanc, and bring in a very late grape harvest in the cold autumn.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
