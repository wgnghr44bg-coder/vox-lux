Drift off to ancient Rome in the winter and spring of 44 BC, the last months of Julius Caesar, and discover what ordinary Romans actually did each day. A calm history for sleep.
Wake with the bakers at dawn, lie on a couch at a Roman dinner party, visit the steaming baths and the chariot races, read Cicero's letters by lamplight, meet Cleopatra across the river and follow the Ides of March gently and from a distance, to the flowers that people still leave for Caesar in the Forum today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, deep and warm 432 Hz music in
the background and softly changing images.
