Drift off to the story of ancient Rome in the year 44 BC, the greatest city in the world, in the winter and spring when Julius Caesar died. A calm history for sleep.
Walk through the Forum and the crowded streets, spend ordinary days with the people of Rome, visit the baths and the races, read the letters of Cicero, meet Cleopatra and Brutus, follow the Ides of March gently and from a distance, and see the places where it all happened as they are today.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
