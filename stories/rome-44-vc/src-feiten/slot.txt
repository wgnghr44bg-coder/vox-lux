So our slow walk through Caesar's Rome comes to a close.

Evening settles over the seven hills. The bakers have banked their ovens for the night. In the little round temple, the sacred fire glows softly, tended by a quiet figure in white. Down by the river, the water slides past the bridges, dark and calm. In a hundred small rooms, people blow out their clay lamps, one after another. Somewhere far off, a cart rumbles over the stones, and then it too is gone.

If sleep hasn't found you quite yet, another unhurried story is ready for you on the screen. And should this one have helped you rest, subscribing makes the next walk easy to find.

Let the old city keep its secrets now. Its streets are quiet, and so are yours.

Sleep well, and good night.
