Drift off to the story of daily life in ancient Sparta, around 480 BC, in the green valley of the river Eurotas below the Taygetos mountains. A calm history for sleep about families, farms and festivals.
Meet a Spartan mother who runs her house and farm, her daughter who loves to run and sing in the choir, and her young son who leaves home for the famous agoge. Visit the helot farmers and the craftsmen of Laconia, taste the black broth at the common meals, watch the summer festivals, hear the old laws of Lycurgus and the two kings, and learn how news of Thermopylae reached the quiet valley.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
