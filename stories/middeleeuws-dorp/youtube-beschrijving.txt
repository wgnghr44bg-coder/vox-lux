Drift off to a whole year inside a medieval English village in 1300, the village of Elton beside the river Nene, whose court rolls still tell us the names and little stories of its people. A calm history for sleep.
Walk through the open fields and the meadows, watch the oxen plough and the mowers make hay, visit the mill, the smithy and the church, sit in the manor court, dance around the maypole and the Midsummer bonfire, bring in the harvest, ride to the great fair at St Ives, and visit Elton as it is today, where the old ridge and furrow still lies in the grass.

A calm, slow-paced history documentary for sleep and relaxation. No loud
sounds, no jump scares, just a gentle voice, soft 432 Hz background music
with smooth, gentle rain sounds and softly changing images.
